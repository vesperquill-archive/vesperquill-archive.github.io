Chapter 59

“It would seem the imperial mages have acquired yet another magical creature.”

The Head Maid, who had been following quietly behind Talia, released a weary sigh as she spoke. Talia scarcely paid her any attention. Her gaze had already settled upon the enormous cage standing at one side of the hall. Drawn towards it by a curiosity she could not explain, she slowly approached, her eyes fixed upon the dark shape concealed within.

As she drew nearer, the creature stirred beneath its great, pitch-black wings and slowly raised its head. Talia flinched, her shoulders drawing inward.

The beast possessed the enormous body of a dark-feathered bird, yet where its head ought to have been was the face of a human woman. It was a dreadful sight, for the face appeared less like that of a living person than something pieced together from the remains of the dead. Its pallid skin was stretched unnaturally tight, while thick blue veins stood out beneath the surface.

Then a voice, foul and unpleasant as the stench of rotting flesh, slithered into her ears.

“Does the creature stir some sense of kinship within you?”

Talia turned sharply. Gareth stood directly behind her, dressed in a richly coloured red doublet. A mocking smile curved his lips as he regarded her with undisguised amusement.

“A woman’s face upon the carcass of a monster,” he drawled. “Surely the resemblance must remind you of someone rather familiar?”

His eyes narrowed as they travelled slowly down her figure, lingering upon the legs concealed beneath the heavy folds of her gown. The Crown Prince’s smile broadened.

“I have heard that your injuries have left you with a most hideous scar. How fortunate you are, then, that yours may be hidden beneath a gown. That wretched beast, alas, enjoys no such luxury.”

A bitter taste surged into the back of Talia’s throat. Before she could think better of it, she spun around and struck the exalted Crown Prince across the face. The sharp sound rang through the hall.

A shrill cry immediately followed, resembling the squeal of a pig being led to slaughter. It was most likely the Head Maid, who had been following Talia about like a prison guard. Out of the corner of her eye, Talia caught a glimpse of the woman staggering backwards, apparently too horrified even to breathe.

But Talia paid her no attention. Her gaze remained fixed upon Gareth. With a sudden movement, she reached for his face again, dragging her fingernails down his bronze-coloured cheek. The nails, still bruised and damaged beneath their beds, tore across his skin, leaving four bright red scratches stretching from beneath his eye towards his jaw. Blood began to seep from the wounds.

Yet even that was not enough to satisfy her. Talia lunged towards him once more, determined to inflict greater damage, but the guards reacted before she could reach him. They rushed forward and seized both her arms.

She struggled violently, twisting and pulling against their grip like a wild animal caught in a trap. But the soldiers held her firmly, refusing to release her. Tears of helpless fury sprang to her eyes. Through her blurred, bloodshot vision, she glared at her half-brother.

He stood before her with his broad shoulders, his powerful frame, and the healthy body that had never known the suffering she now endured. Everything about him seemed offensively perfect.

No...

Her eyes settled upon the blood trickling down his cheek.

Not anymore.

A shrill laugh escaped her lips.

“Come now, dear brother. Say those words once more, if you dare. I shall tear that handsome face of yours to pieces until you are no longer capable of smiling so smugly!”

She laughed harder, scarcely caring that the guards were still restraining her.

“Do you imagine I have anything left to lose? There is nothing you can take from me now, Gareth! And a woman with nothing left to lose has nothing left to fear!”

The Crown Prince had been staring at her in stunned disbelief, one hand pressed against his wounded cheek. Now his expression twisted into something vicious. A moment later, his hand shot forward and seized her by the hair.

He caught hold of the elaborate braids that had taken the maids hours to arrange and wrenched them backwards with such force that Talia feared her neck might snap.

“You insolent little wretch...” he hissed, his face streaked with blood and flushed a dreadful shade of crimson. “How dare you? How dare a miserable creature such as yourself presume to lay a hand upon my face?”

The pain was excruciating. Yet Talia only laughed louder. She could see the last remnants of reason disappearing from his darkening eyes, and the sight filled her with a reckless, almost delirious satisfaction.

Gareth seized her face with his other hand, pressing down with such brutal force that it seemed he intended to crush her skull between his palms. Agony shot through her head, her vision beginning to blur beneath the unbearable pressure. Still, she refused to stop laughing.

Then, without warning, another hand intervened. A powerful grip closed around Gareth and tore him away from her.

Released so suddenly, Talia collapsed upon the cold stone floor. For several moments, she remained where she had fallen, struggling to draw breath as the world swam before her eyes. Gradually, her vision cleared, and a familiar figure came into view.

Talia blinked, unable at first to comprehend what she was seeing. Then she hurriedly pushed herself upright, her heart pounding so violently that she could hear the blood rushing through her ears. Almost instinctively, her trembling hands flew to her skirts, pulling the fabric down to ensure that her legs remained completely concealed.

Above her, a low voice broke the silence.

“Ah, so you have finally decided to show yourself. I had begun to wonder how much longer you intended to remain out of sight.”

Gareth wrenched his wrist free from the iron grip that had restrained him. His teeth ground together audibly, and his dark green eyes seemed almost black with fury.

“Have you truly lost all sense of loyalty? Do you mean to cast us aside for the sake of that girl?”

Varkas stood before him, his expression composed. After a brief pause, he replied in a measured voice.

“...His Imperial Majesty has entrusted me with the duty of escorting the Second Princess into his presence.”

“Again with those wretched excuses! Do you take me for a fool? I—”

“Your Highness shall have my answer once my audience with His Imperial Majesty has concluded.”

Varkas’s words were calm, but they left no room for argument. Gareth’s bloodstained face contorted with rage. A harsh sound escaped his clenched jaw as he ground his teeth so fiercely that they seemed in danger of breaking.

Then he seized the future Grand Duke by the shoulder and leaned towards him.

“Very well, then. I shall await your explanation with the greatest interest. And I advise you to consider most carefully what answer you intend to give me.”

With that, he released Varkas and turned away. The gathered onlookers hurriedly moved aside as the Crown Prince pushed through them, his expression murderous. A moment later, he disappeared into the corridor.

Varkas watched his retreating figure for several seconds before finally turning his attention towards Talia. A cold shiver passed over her skin. The fine hairs at the back of her neck rose as she felt his gaze settle upon her.

Those dark, unfathomable eyes had always made her feel painfully insignificant, as though she were something so small and unworthy that he could scarcely be troubled to acknowledge her existence. Now, reflected within their depths, she seemed to see the full extent of her own miserable condition: dishevelled hair, trembling limbs, and a body that could scarcely support itself. The weight of his gaze felt almost unbearable.

Talia lowered her eyes. Planting her shaking palms against the stone floor, she gathered what little strength remained in her legs and attempted to stand. But before she could rise, a firm hand slipped beneath her back.

She startled. Varkas had crouched beside her. As he leaned closer, Talia instinctively recoiled from his chest.

“P-please... Do not touch me!”

Her protest was scarcely more than a frightened whisper. It made no difference. With effortless strength, Varkas slipped one arm beneath her knees and the other around her back. Then he lifted her from the floor.

Talia nearly screamed. Her hands immediately flew towards her skirts, clutching desperately at the fabric.

My legs...!

For one dreadful moment, she feared that the movement had exposed the ruined flesh she had struggled so desperately to conceal. She looked down in panic. The heavy folds of her gown still covered her legs completely, reaching all the way to the tips of her shoes. Yet even after confirming this, the terror within her refused to subside.

Her fingers remained tightly curled around the hem of her skirt, their knuckles turning white.

“I... I am perfectly capable of walking, my lord. Pray, put me down.”

Varkas did not answer. He merely straightened and began walking. Talia swallowed with difficulty. His silence seemed to tighten around her throat like a noose.

“Did you not hear me? I demand that you put me down this instant!”

Her voice rose sharply. At once, she felt the arms supporting her tighten ever so slightly. Talia looked up. The sight of his expression caused the rest of her protest to die upon her lips. There was something dark and tightly restrained in his face, something she could neither understand nor bear to examine too closely.

Varkas kept his gaze fixed straight ahead. When he finally spoke, his voice was low and rough.

“Surely Your Highness has no desire to invite any further attention upon yourself than you have already endured.”

Talia stared at him.

“Allow me to carry you a little farther. It would spare you the indignity of stumbling before the eyes of this entire assembly.”

For several seconds, she could only look at the firm line of his jaw. Then, slowly, she turned her head. The corridor was filled with people. Dozens of wide, astonished eyes were fixed upon her. Some appeared horrified, while others stared with undisguised curiosity. Everywhere she looked, someone was watching.

Talia’s face burned. She immediately lowered her head, tucking her chin against her chest as though she might somehow disappear from their sight. A faint sigh stirred the hair at the crown of her head. She bit her lower lip so fiercely that she tasted blood. The urge to weep was almost unbearable, but she refused to allow the tears to fall.

“Only a little farther, Your Highness. I ask that you endure it for a moment longer.”

Varkas murmured the words so quietly that only she could hear them. Then he quickened his pace.

Before long, the grand double doors of the imperial audience chamber came into view. The chamberlain stationed outside stood rigid with astonishment when he saw the future Grand Duke approaching with the Second Princess in his arms. For a moment, the man seemed to forget his duties entirely.

Varkas paid no attention to his bewilderment. He merely raised his chin slightly.

“I have come in obedience to His Imperial Majesty’s summons. See that the doors are opened.”

The chamberlain immediately recovered himself.

“Y-yes, my lord! At once!”

He hurried to obey, pulling apart the enormous doors with the assistance of the attendants. Beyond them lay the magnificent audience chamber, its vast interior illuminated by brilliant light. At the far end of the hall stood the imperial throne, gleaming with gold.

Talia raised her eyes towards it. Seated upon the throne was a man of imposing stature, his powerful frame giving him the appearance of a monument carved from stone. There was something overwhelming about his presence, an air of absolute authority that seemed to fill the entire chamber.

Yet his expression was one of weary indifference. He was looking down at a sheet of parchment, apparently more interested in its contents than in anything happening around him.

Beside him stood Senevier. The Empress had been speaking quietly to her husband, but the sound of the opening doors caused her to turn. Her luminous, pale face brightened with an immaculate smile.

“Ah, there you are at last. We have been awaiting your arrival.”

Only then did the Emperor lift his gaze. His eyes settled upon Talia. She instinctively moistened her dry lips. There was no warmth in the look he gave her. His heavy, indifferent gaze travelled slowly down the length of her body, examining her as though she were some troublesome object brought before him for inspection.

Talia felt her humiliation deepen. She could endure no more. Turning sharply in Varkas’s arms, she began struggling against his hold.

“Put me down, my lord. I insist that you release me at once.”

But Varkas ignored her. He continued walking towards the raised platform upon which the imperial throne stood. Talia looked up at him in bewilderment.

What in the world is he thinking?

She could not understand why he was behaving so recklessly. He was Ayla’s betrothed, was he not? How could he possibly think it appropriate to carry another princess into the imperial audience chamber, directly before the Emperor and Empress?

Does he truly believe he may behave in such a manner before His Imperial Majesty?

Her anxiety deepened. She turned her gaze back towards the throne. At that moment, the Emperor’s stern voice descended upon them.

“I was given to understand that your injuries had not impaired your ability to walk. Was I misinformed?”

The words struck Talia with the force of a reprimand. Her shoulders stiffened. Almost immediately, an excuse tumbled from her lips.

“I merely lost my footing along the way, Your Majesty. Lord Sierkhan was gracious enough to come to my assistance.”

As she spoke, she pressed both hands firmly against Varkas’s broad chest, urging him to release her. For a moment, he remained as immovable as granite. Then, at last, he bent and carefully lowered her onto the marble floor.
