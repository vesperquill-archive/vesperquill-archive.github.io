Chapter 44

Talia pushed through the soldiers who were hurriedly gathering their weapons, her eyes searching desperately for Varkas. The campsite had fallen into dreadful chaos. Tents lay in ruins, broken equipment was scattered everywhere, and among the wreckage were the bodies of unfortunate soldiers and servants, so terribly injured that they were scarcely recognisable. The sight made her stomach tighten with fear, yet she could not bring herself to stop. The thought that Varkas might meet the same terrible fate frightened her far more than the horrors surrounding her.

She hurried across the ruined camp, stumbling over pieces of wood and abandoned belongings as she struggled to catch up with him. Varkas had already gone far ahead, and no matter how quickly she ran, the distance between them seemed impossible to close. Then a heavy impact shook the ground, followed by a dreadful noise that made her turn her head.

Not far away, an enormous wyvern had landed among the soldiers and was swallowing one of them whole. Its monstrous jaws moved repeatedly, crushing its unfortunate victim as blood spilled from its mouth and ran down the dark scales of its body. Talia stood motionless, unable to comprehend such a horrifying sight. Her legs trembled so violently that she nearly collapsed, and she took a frightened step backwards.

Then she spotted Varkas among a line of knights who were preparing their spears. He was issuing orders with his usual authority, seemingly undisturbed by the terrible danger surrounding him. The moment Talia recognised him, she forgot everything else and cried out with all the strength she could muster.

"Varkas! Varkas!"

She ran towards him like a frightened child who had finally found someone familiar in a crowded place. All she wanted was to reach him and remain somewhere safe by his side. Hearing her voice, Varkas turned from the knights he had been commanding. His eyes widened slightly when he saw her, but his expression quickly hardened into anger. He was clearly displeased that she had disobeyed his instructions and followed him into the battlefield.

Ordinarily, his cold and disapproving gaze would have made Talia hesitate. Yet she continued running towards him, her thoughts consumed by a single desperate wish.

Please, don't fight anymore. Leave the monsters to your men. You need only stay beside me. Just stay where I can see you.

She knew how unreasonable such a demand would sound. Varkas was responsible for protecting the imperial procession, and he could hardly abandon his duties simply because she was frightened. Nevertheless, she wanted him to remain with her, away from the terrible creatures that threatened to take his life.

Although she had spoken none of these thoughts aloud, Varkas began walking towards her as though he understood what she wanted. Relief stirred within her chest, and she immediately reached out her hand.

At that very moment, however, a faint scream rose from another part of the battlefield.

"Varkas!"

His head turned towards the voice without hesitation. Talia followed his gaze and saw Ayla standing alone amid the destruction. The princess appeared to have become separated from her escort during the confusion and was stumbling helplessly across the ruined ground. Above her, an enormous shadow was descending.

Before Talia could even understand what was happening, Varkas turned away and rushed towards Ayla.

Talia stopped in her tracks, her outstretched hand still suspended in the air. She watched him hurry away from her, and a terrible ache spread through her chest.

Don't go.

Please... don't go to her.

The words remained trapped in her throat. She wanted to call him back, to beg him not to leave her standing there alone, but her voice refused to come. A painful sob rose within her, burning her throat as she struggled to breathe.

Varkas reached Ayla just as the wyvern descended upon her. He caught the princess in his arms and threw himself aside, rolling across the ground barely a moment before the creature's enormous claws struck the place where they had been standing. Having missed its prey, the monster released a furious roar and tore at the earth with its sharp black talons.

Varkas quickly rose and drew his sword, placing Ayla behind him as he prepared to defend her. Yet the creature did not immediately attack again. Instead, its enormous head moved from side to side, its nostrils flaring as though it were searching for another scent. Then its glowing crimson eyes settled upon Talia.

She froze beneath its terrible gaze. The creature's pupils widened, almost as though it had discovered a more desirable prey, and before she could move, its enormous body rushed towards her with astonishing speed.

The attack came so suddenly that Talia had no time even to scream. A pair of sharp talons closed around her body, and in the next instant, she was violently lifted from the ground.

Her stomach lurched as the earth fell away beneath her. She looked down in terror, watching the campsite grow smaller and smaller until the soldiers below appeared no larger than the palm of her hand. Panic overwhelmed her, and she began struggling desperately, kicking her legs and twisting her body in an attempt to escape.

But the more she struggled, the tighter the monster's grip became. Its enormous claws pressed painfully against her chest, crushing her ribs until she could scarcely breathe. She feared that the sharp talons would pierce straight through her body and tear her apart.

Talia raised her head towards the creature's monstrous face, her eyes filling with tears.

"H-help me...! Someone... please help me!"

Her voice was so faint that even she could barely hear it above the rushing wind. She tried to call out again, forcing air into her aching lungs, but before another word could escape her, a brilliant flash of light cut through the darkening sky.

A wave of scorching heat swept across her face, making her scream in terror. The wyvern released a piercing shriek and began convulsing violently in the air. Its talons tightened around her with such force that she thought her bones would shatter. An unbearable pain spread through her body, and her vision turned white.

She had no strength left to struggle. Her arms and legs went limp, and for a moment, she could scarcely remain conscious. Then, without warning, the creature's grip loosened, and Talia was thrown into the empty air.

She fell helplessly through the darkness, the wind rushing past her ears as the ground rose rapidly to meet her. There was nothing she could grasp and no way to protect herself. After several dreadful seconds, her body struck something with tremendous force.

A terrible pain tore through her, and everything went black.

For some time, Talia could not tell whether she was awake or unconscious. Her thoughts drifted through the darkness, and she wondered vaguely whether she had already died. Perhaps the fall had killed her, and this strange emptiness was all that remained.

But then an agonising wave of pain swept through her body, cruelly reminding her that she was still alive.

A low groan escaped her lips as she struggled to breathe. Every breath came in short, painful gasps, and her entire body seemed to burn with an agony she could scarcely understand. It hurt so terribly that she could not even tell which part of her had been injured.

"Ah... ugh..."

She tried to crawl, dragging herself blindly across the cold earth as though she could somehow escape the pain. But the smallest movement sent another dreadful ache through her limbs. Unable to endure it, she collapsed forward and pressed her forehead against the ground, struggling desperately for breath.

Then a sharp, unpleasant smell reached her nostrils. Talia slowly lifted her head and forced her eyes to adjust to the darkness. At first, she could make out nothing more than a large, shapeless mass lying nearby. Gradually, however, she recognised the enormous body of the wyvern.

The creature was dead.

Talia stared at it through her tears, scarcely believing what she saw. One side of its wings had been burned almost beyond recognition, the black membranes shrivelled and torn as though they had been caught in a great fire. Much of the scales covering its hind legs had been stripped away, and faint red embers still glowed upon its ruined flesh.

She had no idea what had killed the monster or how she herself had survived the fall. For a long moment, she could only stare at the motionless creature in bewilderment. Then she placed her trembling hands against the ground and attempted to lift herself.

Only then did she discover why she could not move properly.

One of her legs was trapped beneath a heavy mound of earth and fallen rocks. She appeared to have tumbled down a steep embankment during the fall, bringing loose stones and soil down upon herself.

I have to get out of here...

Talia twisted her body and tried desperately to pull her leg free. But the moment she moved, a dreadful pain shot through her lower body, forcing a strangled cry from her throat. She collapsed again, unable to endure the agony.

It was not merely her trapped leg that hurt. Her chest, shoulders, and arms were all terribly painful, and every breath seemed to send sharp aches through her ribs. She wondered whether every bone in her body had been broken. It certainly felt that way, and she could scarcely understand how she was still breathing after such a fall.

Perhaps I'm not truly surviving. Perhaps I'm simply dying very slowly.

The thought filled her with terror. She looked around desperately, hoping to see some sign of another person, but darkness surrounded her on every side. The hollow into which she had fallen seemed completely separated from the campsite above, and the last traces of daylight were rapidly disappearing.

For a dreadful moment, Talia wondered whether this was what it felt like to be buried alive. The darkness seemed to press against her, and drawing breath became more difficult with every passing moment.

She wished she could lose consciousness. If only she could faint, perhaps she would no longer have to endure such unbearable pain. Yet strangely, her senses seemed to grow sharper instead. She could feel every stone beneath her body, every movement of her injured limbs, and every terrible throb that passed through her.

Unable to bear it any longer, Talia dug her fingers into the earth. Her nails were already torn, but she continued scraping helplessly at the dirt while tears streamed down her face.

"It hurts..." she whispered. "It hurts so much..."

Her voice trembled, and before she even realised what she was saying, another name escaped her lips.

"Varkas..."

She did not know why she had called for him. Perhaps it was because some foolish part of her still believed that he would come if she needed him badly enough. Perhaps, despite everything, he was still the person she wanted most whenever she was frightened.

Then the memory returned.

Varkas turning away from her. Varkas rushing towards Ayla without hesitation. Varkas taking her half-sister into his arms while Talia stood alone, her hand still stretched towards him.

The recollection brought fresh tears to her eyes.

I will never matter to him.

She had always known it, hadn't she? To Varkas, the people who truly deserved his protection were Ayla and Gareth. They were the ones whose safety mattered above everything else, the ones he would risk his life to save.

And Talia was merely someone he had been ordered to tolerate.

"No..." she whispered shakily. "No, that isn't true..."

She swallowed painfully, trying to calm herself.

"He'll come for me."

The words sounded uncertain even to her own ears, but she repeated them as though saying them aloud might somehow make them true.

"He will... He must..."

After all, she was still the Emperor's daughter. Whatever Varkas might think of her personally, he was a man who placed duty above all else. Surely he would not abandon an imperial princess in such a dreadful situation.

Even if he cared nothing for her life, he would at least come to recover her body.

He wouldn't simply leave me here... would he?

Talia fixed her gaze upon the darkness, desperately hoping to see someone approaching. She listened for footsteps, for voices, for anything that might suggest the soldiers were searching for her.

But no one came.

The faint embers still glowing upon the wyvern's ruined body gradually began to fade. One by one, their reddish light disappeared until even the enormous carcass was swallowed by darkness.

Soon, the world around her became completely black.

Still, Talia waited.

Her throat had grown painfully dry, and every attempt to speak made it ache. Nevertheless, she forced herself to whisper a few comforting words.

"It's all right... Everything will be all right. He'll come soon. I'm certain he will..."

She paused, struggling to swallow.

"Just a little longer... He'll be here soon..."

But her voice broke, and the words dissolved into a miserable sob.

Talia immediately pressed her trembling hand over her mouth. She did not want to cry. She could not allow herself to cry, for she feared that once she began, she would never be able to stop.

If I cry, it will mean I've accepted it.

Her chest tightened painfully.

It will mean that no one is coming to save me...

She bit her lower lip and struggled to swallow the sobs rising in her throat. The pain was unbearable, and the darkness seemed endless, yet she continued telling herself that she needed only to endure a little longer.

Varkas would surely come.

He had saved her before. Once, when she had been trapped in the mire, he had reached for her and pulled her free. Surely he would do so again.

He would find her beneath the fallen earth and stones. He would take her away from this dreadful place, and she would no longer have to suffer alone in the darkness.

Talia clung desperately to that fragile hope, repeating the same promise within her heart as the silence deepened around her.

He'll come.

He must...

Surely...
