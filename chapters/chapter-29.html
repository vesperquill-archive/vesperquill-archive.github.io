Chapter 29

Talia nervously played with her fingers before finally speaking. Her voice sounded unusually quiet and strained.

"Be gentle with my hair. If you damage even one strand, I won't forgive you."

Varkas did not answer, but his movements became noticeably more careful. As he worked to free the strands caught in his sleeve, Talia swallowed nervously. The gentle movements of his fingers made her increasingly aware of how close they were sitting.

From where she sat, she could see the open collar of his shirt and the strong lines of his neck. Every movement of his arms reminded her of his presence, while the warmth of his body made it difficult for her to remain calm. Embarrassed by her own thoughts, Talia quickly lowered her head to hide her flushed cheeks.

"Are you nearly finished?" she asked after a while, unable to bear the silence any longer.

She wondered why something as simple as untangling a few strands of hair was taking so much time. Perhaps only a few minutes had passed, but her nervousness made the moment feel much longer. Her palms had grown damp with sweat, so she discreetly wiped them against the clothes scattered across the floor.

When she noticed that even her wrists had turned pink, she became more embarrassed. What if her entire face was just as red? The thought of Varkas noticing made her panic, and she immediately raised her voice.

"Never mind! Just cut whatever is tangled and be done with it!"

Varkas, who had been patiently examining the strands, finally stopped what he was doing. He reached towards his waist and drew a small dagger. Seeing the blade, Talia immediately stiffened. When he moved his hand behind her head, she instinctively grabbed his sleeve.

"Wait! Don't cut off too much! My hair is—"

Before she could finish speaking, a quiet snip interrupted her. The uncomfortable pulling sensation against her scalp disappeared almost immediately, and Talia hurriedly turned around, terrified that he might have cut off a large section of her hair.

Fortunately, there were no fallen strands on the carpet. Instead, a small golden button lay near her feet. She picked it up and examined it curiously, noticing the finely engraved emblem of the Roem Knight Order on its surface.

When she looked up again, Varkas had already risen to his feet and was returning the dagger to its sheath.

"Do you intend to stay on the floor all evening?" he asked, straightening his slightly disordered clothes.

Talia slowly stood, feeling strangely awkward. She cleared her throat and held out the button towards him.

"Here. You should take this back."

"There's no need. You can throw it away," Varkas replied without much interest.

He turned towards the window, where the afternoon sunlight had already begun fading into the warm colours of sunset. Talia had been so distracted that she had not noticed how much time had passed. After looking around the room and seeing the dresses and accessories scattered everywhere, Varkas released a tired sigh.

"If there's nothing else you require, may I leave now?"

Talia nodded without saying anything. Varkas gave a small bow before walking out of the room, leaving her standing alone among the discarded clothes.

She remained still for a while, listening to his footsteps disappear down the corridor. Then, as soon as she was certain he had gone, she hurried towards her bed and pulled out her jewellery box.

Opening it carefully, Talia placed the golden button at the very bottom, beneath her other treasured belongings. She had no intention of throwing it away, regardless of what Varkas had said.

That night, Talia found herself unable to sleep. A strange excitement filled her chest, leaving her restless and confused. No matter how hard she tried to calm herself, she kept remembering everything that had happened that afternoon. The way Varkas had spoken to her, the careful movements of his hands, and the brief moments when their eyes had met all returned to her mind again and again.

Perhaps he really does care about me. No, that can't be right. But what if he does?

Her thoughts continued going back and forth until she felt as though she might lose her mind. Yet despite her confusion, she could not stop smiling. She spent the entire night tossing and turning, holding on to the small happiness she had found in that ordinary afternoon.

Unfortunately, those hopeful dreams did not last long. Only a few days later, news spread throughout the Imperial Palace that Varkas was officially engaged to Princess Ayla.

For Talia, it felt as though she had suddenly awakened from a beautiful dream. No, it was far worse than that. One moment, she had been floating among the clouds, imagining a future that might never exist. The next, she felt as though she had fallen straight to the ground.

She could hardly believe what she had heard. Without wasting another moment, Talia rushed out of the Separate Palace and headed towards the training grounds. Varkas would normally come to attend to her at noon as part of his duties as her royal guard, but she could not possibly wait until then. She needed to see him immediately.

She hurried across the Imperial Palace, barely paying attention to the people she passed. By the time she reached the training grounds, she was almost out of breath. Several knights turned to look at her in surprise when she entered without warning, but Talia paid no attention to their cautious expressions.

Her eyes searched desperately among the men gathered there, looking for Varkas's familiar pale ash-blond hair. However, she soon realised that most of those present were trainees rather than the regular knights. If Varkas was not training, he was probably attending to paperwork in his office.

With that thought, she immediately turned towards the military offices beside the training grounds. As she had expected, Varkas was there, but he was not alone.

Talia had just reached for the door handle when she heard a faint sound coming from inside. Someone was crying. Her hand stopped, and she cautiously looked through the narrow opening of the door.

Varkas stood near the window, while a young woman stood close to him with her face buried against his chest. The woman was crying so desperately that Talia could hear her trembling breaths from the corridor.

Talia froze. She had never imagined witnessing such a scene, and for a moment, she could not understand what she was seeing. Before she could decide whether to leave or announce her presence, the woman slowly lifted her head and looked up at Varkas with tearful eyes.

"Please tell me the truth. You don't actually have feelings for the princess, do you?"

Her voice trembled with desperation as she clung to him, seemingly afraid that he might walk away.

"This marriage is only happening because of politics, isn't it? Please, just tell me that's the reason you're agreeing to marry her."

Talia felt her throat tighten. There was another woman who cared for Varkas, someone who could stand before him and openly beg him to explain his feelings. The discovery struck her so suddenly that she could barely think.

She watched Varkas anxiously, waiting for his answer. At last, he spoke in the same calm voice she knew so well.

"I don't understand why my personal feelings should matter to you."

Talia instinctively drew her shoulders inward. His voice was cold, and his expression revealed nothing. He looked down at the woman as though he genuinely could not understand why she was asking such questions.

"Whether the engagement was arranged for political reasons makes no difference. I have already agreed to marry Her Imperial Highness, Princess Ayla, and I intend to honour that agreement."

The woman's shoulders stiffened. Even from where she stood, Talia could see how deeply those words had hurt her. However, Varkas did not stop speaking.

"I'm afraid you've misunderstood our relationship. I made it clear from the beginning that I would never be able to return your feelings."

The woman went pale, but Varkas continued in the same composed manner, seemingly unaware of how painful his words sounded.

"I would prefer that you stop approaching me about this matter. My engagement has already been formally announced, and I have no intention of allowing unnecessary rumours to arise."

The woman slowly stepped backwards, looking as though all her strength had disappeared. Unable to remain standing, she sank to the floor. For a brief moment, an expression of irritation crossed Varkas's face.

Talia felt a terrible chill. There was no sympathy in his eyes, no hesitation or regret, and not even the slightest sign that he felt sorry for the woman crying before him.

That expression was painfully familiar. It was the same cold face he had shown Talia countless times, and suddenly, she could no longer bear to watch.

She turned and hurried away from the office, her heart pounding violently. As she walked, a frightening thought entered her mind.

If I had arrived just a little earlier, perhaps I would have been the one standing there.

If she had reached him before that woman, perhaps she would have been the one desperately asking whether he could ever love her. Perhaps she would have been the one left crying on the floor, unable to endure his answer.

Just imagining it made her shudder. If Varkas had looked at her with those same indifferent eyes while she confessed her feelings, Talia did not think she could have endured it. The humiliation and heartbreak would have been unbearable.

She knew with frightening certainty that she would have been completely devastated.

That was the moment Talia began to feel truly afraid of Varkas. Until then, she had feared losing him and dreaded the day he would finally leave her side. But now, she realised there was something even more frightening than their separation.

He could destroy every hope she had carefully hidden in her heart with nothing more than a few words.

From that day forward, Talia became even more guarded around him. She could no longer allow herself to see Varkas simply as the person she secretly loved. Instead, he had become someone she needed to protect her heart from.

If she failed to hide her feelings, she feared that every moment spent near him would only bring her more pain.
