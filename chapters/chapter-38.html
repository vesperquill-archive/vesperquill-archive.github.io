Chapter 38

"Release me at once!"

Talia raised her foot and aimed another sharp kick at Gareth's shin, hoping to escape as she had done that morning. This time, however, her brother was prepared. He easily moved out of the way before catching her arm and twisting it painfully behind her back. A cruel smile spread across his face as he watched her struggle.

"I suspected there was something peculiar about your behaviour, but I never imagined this," Gareth said with a mocking laugh. "To think that the greatest disgrace of the imperial family has secretly set her heart upon the future Duke of Sierkhan. How utterly ridiculous!"

Talia stared at him in horror. For one dreadful moment, she felt as though the blood had frozen in her veins. She had never imagined that Gareth would discover the one secret she had guarded so desperately. Nevertheless, she forced herself to remain composed.

"Don't be absurd. Whatever gave you the impression that I would ever—"

"Absurd indeed!" Gareth interrupted, laughing more loudly. "An illegitimate princess daring to desire a man of Varkas's standing! Have you no sense of your own position?"

He appeared delighted by his discovery. For years, Talia had been a constant source of irritation to him, and now he had finally found something that could wound her more deeply than any ordinary insult.

Gareth seized her chin and forced her to look up at him. The unpleasant smell of wine filled her nostrils as he leaned closer, his expression twisted with contempt.

"I suppose one cannot expect better from someone of your blood. After all, what else could come from the daughter of a common harlot? Naturally, you'd be shameless enough to covet a man who belongs to another woman."

Talia's eyes burned with hatred. She stared directly into his face, refusing to lower her gaze despite the humiliation of his words. Gareth merely smiled, clearly enjoying her anger. He released her chin with a rough shove before continuing in a falsely sympathetic voice.

"You would do well to abandon such foolish dreams, dear sister. A man like Varkas is far beyond the reach of someone like you."

Talia steadied herself and slowly raised her head.

"How curious, Brother. You seem to have forgotten something rather important."

Gareth's smile faltered slightly.

"You speak so freely of my mother's humble birth," she continued, her voice growing colder, "yet the blood in my veins does not belong to her alone. I am also the daughter of a man who amused himself with an innocent young girl while his lawful wife was carrying his child."

The amusement vanished from Gareth's face. Talia watched his expression darken and felt a bitter satisfaction stir within her. She knew perfectly well that she ought to stop speaking, but the temptation to wound him in return was too great.

"And if my memory serves me correctly, that same shameless man's blood runs through your veins as well. Does it not?"

Gareth's entire body went rigid. His hands clenched at his sides, and his expression became so threatening that any sensible person would have retreated immediately. Talia, however, had never been particularly sensible when her pride was at stake.

She gave him a small, mocking smile.

"Since you've taken such an interest in uncovering my private affairs, perhaps I ought to return the favour. Shall we discuss one of your secrets instead?"

A flicker of unease passed through Gareth's eyes. Talia noticed it at once. She leaned closer, lowering her voice until it was scarcely more than a whisper.

"There's a rather interesting rumour circulating among the palace maids. They say that His Highness the Crown Prince has a particular fondness for women with dark, honey-coloured hair and eyes as blue as the sea."

The colour drained from Gareth's face. His expression changed so suddenly that Talia knew she had struck precisely where she intended. Beneath his murderous glare, she could see something far more satisfying than anger.

Fear.

Talia's smile deepened.

"It makes one wonder, doesn't it? When you first became old enough to have such improper dreams, whose face occupied your thoughts? Shall I attempt to guess?"

Gareth trembled violently. For a moment, he looked as though he had been struck by lightning. His breathing grew heavy, and the muscles in his jaw tightened until his teeth were nearly grinding together.

Talia knew she had ventured into dangerous territory. She could see the fury building within him, and some distant part of her mind warned that another word might cost her dearly. Yet she could not bring herself to stop.

"There's no need to look so frightened, dear Brother," she murmured with false sweetness. "I am a most devoted sister, after all. Your little secret shall remain safe with me until my dying day."

She paused, allowing the words to settle before delivering the final insult.

"Who would have imagined that the Crown Prince would inherit His Majesty's disgraceful desires quite so faithfully?"

She had scarcely finished speaking when Gareth struck her. The blow came with such force that her head snapped to the side. Talia lost her balance and fell heavily onto the ground, one hand flying to her burning cheek.

For several moments, the world spun around her. A sharp ringing filled her ears, and she struggled to understand what had happened. Before she could recover, Gareth threw himself upon her.

His hands closed around her throat, and he forced her against the earth with the full weight of his body. Talia's eyes widened in terror. She tried to draw a breath, but the pressure around her neck made it impossible. Pain spread through her throat as Gareth's fingers tightened, and her vision began to blur.

She clawed desperately at his arms, digging her nails into his skin while kicking against his much larger frame. But she had barely eaten for days, and what little strength remained in her body was rapidly disappearing.

Gareth seemed entirely consumed by rage. His face was twisted with hatred, and he continued pressing down upon her as though he could no longer hear or see anything beyond his own fury.

Talia struggled until her limbs began to weaken. Her movements grew slower, and the darkness gathering at the edges of her vision became impossible to ignore. At last, her hands slipped from his arms, and her body went almost completely still.

Just as she felt herself slipping into unconsciousness, the crushing pressure suddenly disappeared.

Talia curled onto her side and drew in a desperate breath. Air rushed painfully into her lungs, setting off a violent fit of coughing. Her entire body trembled as she struggled to breathe.

Through her tear-filled eyes, she gradually made out the figure standing above her.

Varkas had arrived.

He was holding Gareth's wrist in a firm grip, preventing the Crown Prince from reaching for her again. For a brief moment, Varkas looked down at Talia. His expression was as cold and unreadable as ever, revealing nothing of what he thought about the scene before him. Then he turned his attention towards Gareth.

The Crown Prince was still glaring at Talia with murderous fury. Even after nearly strangling her, he appeared to regard himself as the injured party, as though her words had justified everything he had done.

The absurdity of it drew a faint, rasping laugh from Talia's throat. Gareth immediately turned towards her. His face darkened to a furious shade of red.

"You miserable little wretch!"

He raised his fist, intending to strike her again. But Varkas stopped him before the blow could fall. Without any visible effort, he caught Gareth's powerful forearm and held it firmly in place. The Crown Prince struggled against him, but Varkas did not move an inch.

His sharp gaze returned briefly to Talia. There was something deeply unpleasant in the way he looked at her. Even after coming so close to losing her life, she was still provoking her brother, and Varkas appeared thoroughly disgusted by her behaviour.

"Take Her Highness back to her quarters," he ordered.

A knight who had hurried into the clearing stood nearby, his face pale from what he had witnessed. At Varkas's command, he immediately approached Talia and offered his hand.

"Your Highness, allow me to assist you."

Talia slapped his hand away.

"I require no assistance."

She forced herself upright, although her legs trembled so badly that she nearly lost her balance. Refusing to show weakness before anyone, she straightened her back and began brushing the dirt from her skirts.

Her cheek throbbed painfully, and every breath irritated her injured throat. Nevertheless, she behaved as though nothing particularly serious had occurred.

Gareth watched her with burning hatred.

"Release me this instant!" he shouted at Varkas.

Before the situation could worsen, another voice rang through the clearing.

"Brother, enough!"

Ayla hurried towards them, her face pale with shock. She had been standing a short distance away, apparently too stunned by the violence to intervene until now. She looked from Talia's dishevelled appearance to Gareth's furious expression, struggling to comprehend what she had witnessed.

"What in heaven's name has come over you?" Ayla demanded. "You are the Crown Prince of this Empire! How could you behave in such a disgraceful manner?"

"Keep out of this!" Gareth snarled.

Ayla stopped abruptly. For a moment, she appeared genuinely frightened. Gareth had always treated her with affection and had rarely shown her anything but devotion. To see him turn upon her with such hostility was plainly something she had never expected.

Talia noticed her reaction and gave a bitter smile.

"For once, Brother has spoken wisely. This matter does not concern you, dear Sister."

Ayla turned towards her, her emerald eyes flashing with displeasure. But when she saw the swelling upon Talia's cheek, the dirt covering her gown, and the marks beginning to appear around her throat, her expression changed.

Talia touched her aching face and smiled faintly.

"There's no reason to look so alarmed. It was merely a small disagreement between siblings. Was it not, Brother?"

She tilted her head towards Gareth, inviting him to agree. The sound of his teeth grinding together was almost audible.

Talia took a step closer to him, ignoring the knight who immediately moved to stand in her way. Her eyes remained fixed upon Gareth's dark green gaze.

"After all, we're such affectionate siblings that we even share our most private secrets. Isn't that right?"

Gareth's face turned an alarming shade of crimson. The hatred in his eyes was so intense that Talia had little doubt he wished to tear her apart where she stood. Yet he could not afford another outburst.

Ayla was watching them. And Talia knew something he would never willingly allow his beloved sister to discover.

For several tense moments, Gareth struggled to master his anger. At last, his shoulders loosened, and he forced the words through clenched teeth.

"Let go of my arm."

Varkas studied him in silence before finally releasing his grip. Dark red marks had already formed around Gareth's wrist, showing just how firmly Varkas had restrained him.

The Crown Prince rubbed his injured arm, casting a venomous glance first at Varkas and then at Talia. Without another word, he turned away and marched towards the campsite.

Talia watched him leave with a quiet, satisfied laugh. For once, Gareth had been forced to retreat.

Her satisfaction, however, lasted only a moment. She suddenly became aware of someone watching her. The amusement vanished from her face as she slowly raised her head.

Varkas stood before her. His expression was as unreadable as ever, but Talia could clearly see the anger beneath his cold composure. There was also something else in his gaze, something that made her stomach tighten.

Disgust.

He looked at her as though her behaviour had offended him beyond words. Before Talia could speak, Varkas reached out and closed his gauntleted hand around her arm.

He then turned towards the nearby knight.

"Escort the First Princess back to her tent."

The knight immediately bowed and moved towards Ayla. Varkas offered Ayla a brief, formal nod before turning away, taking Talia with him.

He began walking through the trees at a brisk pace, pulling her along as though she were a prisoner being escorted to judgment. Talia stumbled after him, struggling to keep up with his long strides.

Her humiliation quickly turned to fury.

How dare he treat me this way?

Whatever he might think of her, she was still a princess of the Empire. Varkas might be the heir of a powerful noble family, but he remained a subject of the imperial throne. He had no right to drag the Emperor's daughter through the camp as though she were some troublesome servant.

Talia pulled violently against his grip.

"Have you forgotten your place? Release me at once, you insolent—"

She never finished the sentence. Without warning, Varkas bent down and lifted her from the ground.

A startled scream escaped Talia as the world suddenly turned upside down. Before she could understand what was happening, he had thrown her over his shoulder as though she weighed no more than a sack of grain.

"Varkas! Put me down this instant!"

Her face burned with humiliation. Varkas ignored her protests and continued walking towards the campsite, his pace steady and entirely unaffected by the furious princess struggling upon his shoulder.

Several soldiers turned to stare as they passed. Their expressions ranged from astonishment to complete disbelief, but Varkas paid them no attention.

Talia could scarcely endure the shame of being carried through the camp in such an undignified manner.

"How dare you! Do you hear me? I order you to put me down!"

She struck his broad back with both fists, then began kicking wildly in an attempt to force him to release her. But Varkas did not so much as flinch.

No matter how fiercely she struggled or how many blows she rained upon his shoulders, he continued forward without a word, carrying her as though her resistance were of no consequence whatsoever.
