Chapter 330

Talia looked up at him with frightened eyes. Over his face, twisted with anger, fell the pitch-black shadow that had made her shudder for years. It was so heavy she could barely even breathe, as if someone were choking her.

She barely managed to squeeze out her voice.

“Move away…”

But rather than backing away, Lucas drew even closer. His hot breath, heavy with the scent of wine, washed over her forehead.

The hairs on Thalia’s body stood on end. She pressed both hands against his chest and shoved him away.

“Didn’t you hear me? I told you to move!”

But he didn’t move an inch. The realization that she couldn’t push him away, no matter how hard she tried, only deepened her fear. Like an animal caught in a trap, Thalia struggled desperately to break free.

“I told you to get away from me! Go away! Just go away!”

As if trying to stop her from struggling, Lucas hurriedly grabbed both her wrists and pinned them against the tree trunk. With her arms restrained, the last shred of reason she had been clinging to finally snapped. Thalia screamed and began kicking his legs with all her might.

“Don’t touch me!”

Her fierce rejection seemed to break the last of his self-control. Lucas looked down at her with bloodshot eyes, then lowered his head and pressed his lips against hers.

Her mind instantly went blank.

She couldn’t even understand what was happening to her. Desperate to escape, she struggled wildly, flailing her arms and legs before biting his lip without mercy. The sickening taste of blood spread through her mouth.

Only then did Lucas seem to regain his senses. He flinched and lifted his head. Thalia seized the opportunity and quickly turned her head to the side.

At that moment, a dark figure rushed in from the edge of her vision. With a heavy thud, Lucas was knocked sideways.

Thalia slumped to the ground, her vision blurred by tears.

Through her tear-blurred vision, she made out the figure of an enormous beast pinning Lucas down with its front paws pressed against his chest.

For a while, she couldn’t fully comprehend what had happened. She stared blankly at Lucas sprawled across the grass and the wolf standing over him. She didn’t know how long she had been staring when a piercing scream rang out from somewhere in the distance.

“Lucas!”

Only upon hearing that desperate, anguished scream did she finally understand what she was seeing. Thalia hurriedly ran toward Khan.

“No!”

Only then did the wolf raise its head and look at her.

Thalia drew in a sharp breath and instinctively took a step back. Blood dripped from the wolf’s mouth.

Her terrified gaze fell upon Lucas, who lay limp beneath the wolf. Bright red blood was gushing from his neck, which had been nearly torn apart.

As she stared blankly at the surreal sight before her, she heard the sound of hurried footsteps. Thalia quickly turned her head and, upon seeing soldiers running toward them from a distance, hurriedly grabbed the wolf and pulled him away.

“Come here!”

At her urgent cry, the wolf, which had been standing as still as a statue, began to move hesitantly.

Thalia urged the reluctant wolf onward and broke into a run. Angry shouts rang out behind her, but she didn’t look back. Ignoring the spasms in her leg, she continued running blindly ahead.

After running for some time, a small gate leading outside finally came into view at the end of the forest path.

She hurriedly rummaged through the inner pocket of her coat. Her fingers closed around the bundle of keys she had placed there some time ago. Hastily sorting through them, she found the key she had used to sneak out of the castle before. She approached the small gate and inserted it into the padlock. Her hands were trembling so badly that it took several attempts before she finally managed to unlock it.

Thalia hurriedly pushed the gate open and ushered Khan outside. Following him through the doorway, she saw the hillside bathed in the fading light of dusk and the Armund Forest stretching beyond it.

With Khan beside her, she immediately ran toward the forest. She nearly lost her footing several times as she made her way down the slope, but she couldn’t afford to slow down. She feared that even the slightest delay would allow the soldiers to catch up with them.

Gasping for breath, she continued running down the hillside. Before long, the birch forest was almost within reach.

She left the narrow path and made her way through the densely packed trees. The hem of her coat caught on thorns and tore, and the bottom of her dress became covered in mud, but she paid no attention. She could feel sweat and tears streaming down her face, soaking it completely, yet she didn’t even have time to wipe them away.

Pushing through thorny bushes and bare branches, Thalia ran breathlessly through the forest. She finally came to a stop when even the pale trunks of the birch trees began to disappear into the bluish darkness. Her legs had grown so weak that she felt she couldn’t take another step.

Clutching her pounding chest with one hand, she struggled to catch her breath. Just then, a loud shout echoed from somewhere in the distance. Startled, Thalia turned to look behind her. Through the densely packed trees, she could faintly see dozens of flickering torches.

The soldiers must have followed them.

Thalia hurriedly pushed Khan, who was standing beside her, away.

“Hurry, go.”

The wolf planted his paws firmly on the ground and let out a low growl. Thalia pushed him away even harder.

“Didn’t you hear me? I told you to go!”

In the darkness, she could see tears slowly welling up in the wolf’s blue eyes as he looked up at her. Tears began streaming down her own cheeks. Thalia picked up a branch lying at her feet and threw it at him.

“Go! Go and never come back!”

The small branch struck his neck, stained with Lucas’s blood, before falling to the ground. But Khan didn’t budge. Instead, he slowly approached her and nudged his head against her.

Thalia looked down at the wolf with anguish in her eyes before finally bursting into tears.

“I told you to go!”

Khan lowered his head and let out a mournful whimper. As though pleading with her, he gently tugged at the hem of her clothes with his teeth. Thalia roughly pushed him away, picked up a small stone, and raised her arm threateningly.

But Khan refused to back away.

She stood glaring at him with wide eyes until she sensed the approaching presence of others. Hardening her heart, she threw the stone. It struck the wolf’s forehead with a dull thud. Khan flinched and took a step back.

Thalia spat out harshly,

“I don’t need you anymore.”

Through her tear-filled eyes, she could see the pain in the wolf’s gaze. Barely managing to keep her expression from crumbling, Thalia forced herself to utter even crueler words.

“I can’t stand the sight of you, so get out of my sight.”

The wolf hesitantly took a step back.

Meanwhile, their pursuers were closing in at an alarming speed. Khan seemed to sense it as well. Crouching low to the ground, he watched the direction from which they were approaching. Thalia picked up another stone and threw it at him.

“Can’t you hear me? I told you to get lost!”

At last, the wolf turned and ran into the densely packed trees. Almost at the same moment, a loud shout rang out from the other side of the forest.

“Over here!”

Thalia instinctively turned around. Soldiers holding torches high were rushing toward her through the trees.

She hurriedly glanced in the direction Khan had disappeared. Thankfully, he was already nowhere to be seen.

But once the guards began searching the area, it might not take them long to discover Khan’s tracks. Hoping to buy him even a little more time, Thalia hurriedly turned around. She forced her trembling legs to move in the opposite direction, but just then, a rough hand suddenly reached out from the darkness.

Thalia swallowed a groan. Turning her head, she found herself staring at the grim face of a knight. The man surveyed her surroundings before speaking in a harsh tone.

“Where is that beast?”

“…I don’t know.”
