Chapter 326

“It's good for your health. Why don't you try some?”

Talia furrowed her brow. She could not understand why this man was offering her such a thing.

With a doubtful expression, Talia glanced between Lucas's sparkling brown eyes and the red berries in his hand. Then she abruptly turned her head away and replied curtly.

“No, thank you. You can eat them yourself.”

A peculiar expression crossed Lucas's face, as though he were both frowning and smiling at the same time. He gave a slight shrug and popped one of the red berries into his mouth.

Talia silently watched his lips move.

With a faint crunch, the clear juice that escaped from the berry moistened his lips. Lucas slowly licked it away with his tongue, then picked up another berry and placed it in his mouth, as though deliberately showing her how delicious it was.

Talia watched him with an indifferent expression as he casually ate the berries, seemingly without any intention of urging her further. Eventually, she turned away and resumed walking along the forest path.

Lucas leisurely followed behind her and asked in a voice tinged with amusement.

“Are you certain you don't want any? They're quite delicious.”

“I already told you I don't.”

Talia replied somewhat irritably, quickening her pace.

However, Lucas continued to follow at a steady distance maintaining a steady distance as he persistently followed her. For some reason, the sound of his footsteps began to bother her.

Talia, who had been stepping over the fallen leaves scattered across the ground while holding a long branch, glanced back once more. In the span of just a few minutes, more than half the berries hanging from the branch had disappeared.

Were they really that delicious?

Talia's eyes followed the bright red berries that swayed enticingly before her. On impulse, she held out her hand.

“……Let me have just one.”

Without a word, Lucas placed the remaining berries in her hand.

Talia examined them from every angle like a suspicious cat before plucking a small berry from the branch and popping it into her mouth.

“Ugh……!”

Talia instinctively squeezed her eyes shut.

The moment she bit into the firm flesh of the fruit, an intensely sour taste spread across her tongue.

An intensely sour taste spread throughout her mouth, so sharp that it was almost unbearable. Having barely managed to swallow the berry, Talia glared at Lucas with a fierce expression.

“You……!”

The man merely shrugged his shoulders with an infuriating smile.

Talia narrowed her eyes at him before snatching up the remaining berries and throwing them in his face.

“Then you can eat the rest!”

The man, who had been clutching his stomach and chuckling as though he found the whole situation terribly amusing, raised both hands in a gesture of surrender.

Talia stared at him in utter disbelief. Then, deciding that he was not worth another moment of her attention, she turned away.

Before she knew it, Khan had wandered quite a distance ahead. To catch up with the wolf, she hurriedly quickened her steps.

When a booming voice suddenly called out from a distance.

“Your Excellency!”

Turning toward the sound, she saw a young man in a red military uniform hurrying toward them, crossing the grassy slope.

His name was Baredon, if she remembered correctly. The knight stopped his horse in front of Lucas and continued, slightly out of breath.

“So this is where you were. I've been looking for you for quite some time.”

“What is it?”

“Didn't you promise to have tea with the guests today? It's already well past noon. You must return to the castle at once.”

The smile vanished from Lucas's face.

He irritably ran a hand through his hair and let out a sigh of frustration.

“What promise? They simply went ahead and made those arrangements among themselves.”

“I understand your displeasure, but you must at least uphold the dignity of the other family.”

The young man spoke with a heavy sigh.

“After all, Her Ladyship came here in accordance with her family's wishes, did she not? You should at least extend her the courtesy due to a guest.”

Lucas frowned at the man's words, looking rather displeased, before finally turning away.

Then, addressing Talia, who had been quietly listening to their conversation, he spoke in a firm tone.

“It seems we will have to return for today. Please call Khan over.”

Talia obediently followed his instructions. Throughout the journey back to the castle, after calling Khan, who had been waiting some distance away, Lucas did not utter another word.

The man who had been teasing her so persistently that she had found him irritating was now walking in silence, his expression deeply subdued. The sudden change in his demeanor felt strangely unfamiliar and awkward.

Talia observed him discreetly as they made their way back to Raedgo Castle. Upon reaching the castle gates, she hurried toward the main building with Khan, as though she had been waiting for the opportunity to leave his side.

Just then, she noticed a young lady standing on the steps leading down to one side of the garden, surrounded by five or six maids and engaged in conversation.

It was Lucas's fiancée.

The young lady wore an elegant green dress, with a pearl ornament adorning her hair. Talia had been gazing absentmindedly at her lovely face when the girl lifted her head and met her eyes.

Although Talia had caught glimpses of her from a distance several times before, this was the first time they had been so close.

Since this was the first time they had come face-to-face at such close quarters, Talia could not help feeling a little startled.

The young lady stared at her blankly for quite some time before leaning toward her maids and whispering something into their ears. Suddenly, Talia was overcome by a strong sense of shame. There was little doubt that they were whispering about the rumors surrounding her.

Talia gazed at the young lady with a flushed face before finally turning away. Passing through a side entrance into the main building, she hurried up the stairs.

The sight of herself sneaking about like a thief, carefully avoiding everyone's gaze, struck her as both pathetic and miserable. Yet she lightly brushed aside the last remnants of her pride, unwilling to become a spectacle for others.

Taking Khan with her, she quietly entered her bedroom.

She approached the chair beside the fireplace, where a fire had been lit, and sank down wearily.

Khan came over to her, rested his head upon her knees, and gazed up at her.

Talia picked up a comb and carefully brushed away the fallen leaves and fragments of leaves clinging to Khan's fur. Then, suddenly noticing that the soles of his paws were stained red, she knelt down on the floor.

Lifting one of Khan's front paws, she examined it closely and noticed that several of his claws were broken.

How did this happen?

Frowning, Talia lifted his other paw and carefully inspected its underside. There, she discovered dark red blood seeping through the cracks of his worn and damaged paw pads.

It looked almost as though he had been clawing desperately at something hard and rough. Talia raised her worried eyes to meet Khan's gaze.

"How did you end up like this?"

Khan lowered his head deeply, avoiding her eyes.

She looked down at him with a troubled expression, then reached for the drawer to fetch some ointment.

As she searched through the drawer, she heard a knock at the door.

“Sister-in-law, may I trouble you for a moment?”

It was Lucas's voice.

Talia frowned. Why had a man who was supposed to be heading straight to the banquet hall followed her all the way here?

With a suspicious expression, Talia reluctantly approached the door and opened it slightly.

“What is it?”

She peered up warily at the man standing outside, revealing only her face. Lucas slipped one hand through the opening and casually pushed his way into the room.

Startled, Talia took a step backward. Lucas, however, remained perfectly composed. The man who had closed the door behind him.

Lucas walked toward the fireplace, dropped heavily into a chair, and let out a long sigh.

“I'll only stay for about an hour before leaving.”

Talia stared at him in disbelief. Her expression quickly darkened, and she raised her voice.

“Stop talking nonsense and leave at once.”

“Please, just let me stay for a little while.”

The man, his broad shoulders slumped, looked up at her with a pleading expression.

“If I go downstairs now, I'll be trapped at that banquet until late into the evening. Please, have pity on this unfortunate fellow and turn a blind eye just this once.”

For a moment, Talia was left speechless by the sheer shamelessness of his behavior.

His almost complete lack of hesitation in asking such a favor softened a small part of her heart, yet at the same time, it stirred a deep sense of wariness within her.

The fact that he had grown so attached to her.

How many times had she already been betrayed by people she had come to trust?

Talia hardened her expression and pointed toward the door.

"Not my problem. Get out of my room right now."

“Isn't that a little harsh?”

Lucas looked at her with the wounded expression of a puppy that had been kicked.

“Haven't I vouched for you every single time so that you could take Khan outside, Sister-in-law? Must you be so cold to me when I ask you for such a small favor in return?”
