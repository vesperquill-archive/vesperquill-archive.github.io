Chapter 323

Eventually, the wolf lowered his head helplessly.

She stepped back hesitatingly. Khan stood completely still, just gazing at her silently.

Was he waiting for her to change her mind?

Perhaps he simply did not want to leave her behind.

After all, if he returned to Raedgo Castle, all that awaited were people's hostile gazes and a suffocating life.

Even if it meant leaving her behind, perhaps he, too, wished to live freely.

Talia slowly lowered her gaze, feeling a corner of her heart grow heavy with sorrow. Just then, she heard a rustling sound.

Lifting her head once more, Talia approached the wolf standing before her and let out a small sigh of relief.

Khan gently raised his head and rested it beneath her chin. Talia lifted both arms and wrapped them around his neck. As the warmth of his body seeped through his damp fur, the anxiety that had been weighing upon her heart gradually began to fade.

Feeling the emotions that had surged within her slowly settle, she wiped the moisture from her wet cheeks.

“……Let's go back now.”

The wolf, who had been watching her with sorrowful eyes, finally turned toward Raedgo Castle.

Talia quietly followed behind him.

Before she knew it, the rain had stopped completely. Sunlight streamed through the clouds, illuminating the vast reed field with a brilliant glow. Whenever the wind blew, golden ripples spread across the entire meadow.

As she slowly crossed the beautiful yet desolate landscape, Talia suddenly broke into a faint smile. For some reason, her sorrow gradually began to melt away, and an inexplicable sense of peace settled within her heart.

Perhaps she had finally grown tired of resisting the emotions that had tormented her for so long. Once she accepted that she could never truly free herself from those feelings, the anguish that had been raging within her grew strangely quiet, almost as though it had been a lie.

Talia slowly retraced the path they had taken, brushing the dry reeds with her fingertips. The meadow gradually receded into the distance, and before long, a dense forest of birch trees stood in her way.

Talia stepped into the forest.

Even though it was broad daylight, the birch forest was eerily dark.

Talia grasped the fur around Khan's neck and carefully climbed the slippery slope. Just then, Khan, who had been walking a step ahead of her, suddenly stopped and raised his head sharply.

Talia held her breath and surveyed their surroundings.

“What's wrong?”

Khan growled, baring his teeth toward the trees. At that very moment, she heard the faint sound of human footsteps in the distance.

Talia stiffened her body. Could a band of looters be camping in the woods? She quickly pulled on the nape of Khan's neck.

“Khan, let's go that way. Hurry.”

Khan stood rigidly, his fur bristling along his back, and glared fiercely at her.

Realizing that Khan was trying to protect her, Talia swallowed hard.

Surely, they aren't trying to ambush us.

As she anxiously stroked Khan's gray fur, the outlines of several men on horseback gradually emerged from between the trees.

Recognizing the emblem of the Bullfram Knights engraved upon their coats, Talia bit her lip, her expression clouded with both relief and unease.

Could they have come looking for me?

No. Perhaps they are merely patrolling the forest in search of bandits.

After pondering for a moment, Talia tightened her grip on Khan's fur. If possible, she wanted to return quietly to the castle without encountering them.

“Let's hurry somewhere else.”

As though he had understood her hurried whisper, Khan quietly turned around.

Talia followed him into a narrow path overgrown with bushes. However, before they had even taken a few steps, from behind them—

Just then, heavy footsteps echoed from behind her, and a rough hand seized her arm.

Talia gasped in surprise and spun around. A broad chest covered in black armor suddenly filled her vision.

Startled, Talia took a step backward and raised her head. Her golden-brown eyes widened as she found herself face-to-face with Lucas, who was struggling to catch his breath.

Perhaps he had been caught in the rain for quite some time, for he had swept his damp black hair back, revealing a face even paler than usual as he looked down at her.

For a moment, her heart seemed to sink.

The sight of Lucas glaring at her with an expression as cold as ice made him look so much like Varkas that it was almost unsettling.

He strode directly toward her and spoke in a sharp voice.

“What on earth are you doing in a place like this?”

Talia stared up at him in bewilderment before suddenly coming to her senses and shaking off his hand.

However, the man's fingers remained firmly wrapped around her wrist, refusing to budge.

Talia glared at him sharply.

“Can't you let go of me this instant?”

"I've been scouring the entire territory for nearly half a day looking for you. And that's all you have to say to me?"

The man spoke through gritted teeth.

“What on earth were you thinking, coming to a place like this without a single guard accompanying you…?”

At that moment, Khan let out a ferocious growl.

Startled, Talia turned her head and saw Khan baring his sharp fangs as he glared at Lucas. She hurriedly pulled her arm away.

“Khan, no!”

However, before Khan could reach him, Lucas roughly pulled Talia behind his back. With his other hand, he reached for the sword hanging at his waist.

Seeing this, Talia cried out sharply, her face turning pale.

“No! If you hurt Khan, I won't let you get away with it!”

Lucas's body instantly went rigid. Glaring at her with his teeth clenched so hard that the veins bulged in his jaw, the man soon took his hand off the sword hilt. However, the hand gripping her wrist still showed no sign of letting go.

Pulling her upright, he informed her in a firm tone.

“First, you must return to the castle.”

Without waiting for her reply, he led her toward the place where the knights were waiting.

Talia had been about to resist him recklessly, but fearing that Khan might be provoked, she obediently followed her brother-in-law.

When they finally reached the place where the horses had been tied, Lucas seized Talia by the waist with both hands and swiftly lifted her onto the saddle.

Then, without giving her even a chance to react, he leaped up behind her with an agile movement and shouted loudly to his men.

“Everyone, return to the castle!”

Almost immediately, Lucas's horse began moving forward.

Only then did Talia recover from her shock and raise her voice in protest.

“What do you think you're doing?! Put me down this instant!”

“Stay still.”

Lucas tightened one arm around her struggling body and growled harshly just above her head.

Feeling his back pressed firmly against hers, Talia instinctively hunched her shoulders.

Even his low, growling voice sounded so much like Varkas's that it sent a chill through her. Feeling a sudden flush of heat rise to her throat, Talia instinctively leaned forward over the saddle.

Lucas looked down at her as though the sight irritated him, then urged his horse forward. Talia hurriedly turned her head to check whether Khan was following them.

Fortunately, the gray wolf was running close behind them. She let out a small sigh of relief at the sight, but her attention was soon drawn to the rough breathing coming from just above her head.

She pulled her body forward on the saddle, hoping to put even a little distance between them. Lucas immediately tightened the arm wrapped around her waist and whispered sharply into her ear.

“If you don't want to fall, stay still.”

Talia, who had been keeping her lips tightly pressed together, suddenly raised her head to retort. However, she froze when she found herself face-to-face with his strikingly handsome, almost sharply sculpted features.

His dark hair, golden eyes, and bronze-colored skin… It was as though Lucas were the complete opposite of Varkas.

Yet why did they look so strangely alike?

Lucas, who had completely shed the traces of his youthful appearance, seemed just as imposing and intimidating as his older brother.

She hurriedly lowered her gaze to conceal the fear that had crossed her face.

“I'll behave, so keep a little distance from me.”

Lucas, who had been frowning, finally loosened the arm wrapped around her waist.

Talia made an effort not to lean against him, gripping the saddle handle tightly in front of her.

How long had they been riding along that winding forest path?

At last, a gentle hill and Raedgo Castle came into view at the edge of the forest.

Talia gazed blankly at the gray stone fortress.

The beautiful yet desolate prison, where not even the slightest glimmer of hope remained, was gradually drawing closer.

Soon, the time would come for her to pay the price for her own reckless actions.
