Chapter 47

Edric raised his voice and shouted towards the approaching knights with all the strength he could muster. "Her Highness is gravely injured! Fetch a healer at once!"

Upon hearing his urgent cry, Sir Theoric Hart immediately turned to the knight behind him and ordered him to summon a mage without delay. Before long, a young palace mage came hurrying through the scattered rocks, his robes brushing against the uneven ground as he approached the injured princess.

While the mage knelt beside Talia to examine her wounds, Edric and the other knights began the difficult task of clearing away the stones that had fallen upon her. Working together, they carefully lifted the enormous slab that had pinned her leg beneath it. As the weight was finally removed, the torn fabric of her skirt slipped aside, revealing the dreadful extent of her injuries.

Edric drew a sharp breath. Her knee and calf had been crushed almost beyond recognition. The flesh was badly torn, and fragments of broken bone protruded through the wounds. He swallowed with difficulty, struggling to remain composed. Even with the assistance of powerful healing magic, an injury of such severity might leave the princess unable to walk properly for the remainder of her life.

The mage examined her leg with a grave expression. After a moment, he spoke in a troubled voice. "If I use healing magic to close these wounds now, the bones may mend in their present position. Her leg would remain terribly deformed, and she might never regain its proper use. We must take her back to camp and set the broken bones before attempting to heal the injury completely."

Edric's expression grew tense. "Will Her Highness survive the journey?"

"I have already cast a restorative spell to stop the worst of the bleeding," the mage replied. "It should keep her condition stable for the time being." He sighed wearily and rubbed the back of his neck before continuing. "However, we cannot move her in this condition without first securing the leg. The broken bones may cause further injury while she is being carried. We shall need something firm enough to serve as a splint."

"Use this," Sir Theoric offered, immediately unfastening the sword scabbard from his waist and handing it to the mage.

The young man accepted it, briefly examining its length and strength before nodding in approval. "This will suffice. I am going to bind her leg now. Please support it carefully from beneath while I work."

Edric obeyed without hesitation. Kneeling beside Talia, he gently slipped both hands beneath her bruised and swollen calf, taking great care not to disturb the broken bones. But the instant his fingers touched her injured leg, a terrible scream tore from her lips.

"AAAAH!"

The sudden cry startled Edric so badly that his hands instinctively loosened. Her broken leg slipped from his grasp, and the exposed bone struck the rough ground. Fresh blood immediately began to seep from the wound, leaving Edric frozen in horror. For a moment, he could do nothing but stare at the terrible injury, overwhelmed by the knowledge that his carelessness had caused her even greater suffering.

Then, without warning, Varkas moved.

The commander, who had been standing silently nearby, dropped to one knee beside the princess. He slipped his arms around her trembling body and drew her firmly against his chest, holding her upper body securely as she struggled in agony.

"Hurry," he ordered in a low, commanding voice. "Stop the bleeding."

His words brought Edric back to his senses. He immediately reached for Talia's injured leg once more, taking care to support it properly this time. Meanwhile, the mage tore a long strip of cloth from a cloak and pressed it firmly against the deep wound along her thigh.

Talia's face was buried against Varkas's chest. Her cries had become broken, desperate gasps, each breath carrying the sound of unbearable pain. Though she was barely conscious, her body continued to struggle instinctively against the agony. Her bloodstained fingers clutched desperately at Varkas's neck, her nails digging into the skin at his nape as though she were trying to hold on to something amid the suffering.

Varkas did not release her. He held her firmly against him while the mage worked, keeping her upper body from moving as the knights prepared the splint.

"That will do!" the mage finally called. "Secure the splint now!"

At his command, the knights quickly knelt beside the princess and fastened Sir Theoric's scabbard along her injured leg, binding it tightly enough to keep the broken bones from shifting. Once the makeshift splint was secured, Varkas carefully gathered Talia's limp body into his arms and rose to his feet.

Edric immediately stepped forward. "Commander, allow me to carry Her Highness. You have sustained injuries yourself, and—"

He extended his hands, intending to take the princess from him. But before he could touch her, Varkas drew Talia closer against his chest and moved slightly away. It was only a small movement, scarcely more than an inch, yet the unmistakable defensiveness of the gesture caught Edric entirely by surprise.

He immediately withdrew his hands.

Varkas's brows drew together in a faint expression of irritation. Without offering any further explanation, he inclined his head towards the path ahead. "Leave her to me. Lead the way. We have already wasted enough time."

"Forgive me, Commander," Edric replied, lowering his head.

He raised his torch and hurried ahead to guide the party out of the ravine. Unfortunately, the rocky cliff was far too steep for them to climb directly while carrying someone in such a delicate condition. They had no choice but to follow the longer, winding path that curved around the edge of the canyon towards the campsite.

Edric led the way through the dark forest, carefully pushing aside the low branches that obstructed their passage. As they walked, he could not resist glancing over his shoulder. The torchlight flickered across Varkas's face, briefly illuminating the sharp line of his jaw and the blood still staining his features.

His expression remained unchanged, yet there was something deeply unsettling about it. It was not merely the cold composure Edric had grown accustomed to seeing upon his commander's face. Rather, Varkas seemed almost entirely absent, as though every trace of feeling had been extinguished from his eyes.

What could possibly be going through his mind?

Edric quickly dismissed the thought. This was neither the time nor the place to concern himself with such matters. The princess needed urgent treatment, and every moment spent in the wilderness placed her in greater danger. Shaking his head, he turned his attention back towards the path and quickened his steps.

✦ ✦ ✦

Talia felt as though her entire leg had been plunged into a blazing furnace. The pain was so fierce that it seemed to burn through her flesh and reach deep into her very bones. A weak moan escaped her lips as she struggled against the suffocating darkness surrounding her.

With considerable effort, she forced her heavy eyelids open. At first, she could see nothing clearly. Everything appeared blurred and strangely distant, as though she were looking through a thick veil of mist. Gradually, however, the pale ceiling of a tent came into view, illuminated by the trembling glow of several candles.

Where... am I?

Confusion clouded her thoughts. She could not remember how she had come to be there. Instinctively, she tried to shift her body, but the moment she moved, a terrible pain shot through her injured leg, so sudden and violent that a scream burst from her throat.

"AAAAH!"

"Hold her still! Do not let her move!" a harsh, unfamiliar voice rang out nearby.

Talia's frightened gaze moved towards the sound. A broad-shouldered, middle-aged man was bending over her injured leg. His expression was grim with concentration, and he held a pair of small iron forceps in his hand. To her horror, she saw him working the instrument deep into the torn flesh around her knee.

Her eyes widened as a wave of terror swept through her so suddenly that she could scarcely breathe.

What is he doing?

She tried to pull her leg away, arching her back and twisting desperately upon the narrow cot. But before she could escape, a pair of strong hands pressed down upon her shoulders from behind.

"Your Highness! Please, you must remain still!"

Talia gasped and turned her head towards the person restraining her. A woman's face hovered above her, thin and exhausted, with an expression strained by worry. For several frantic moments, Talia could not recognise her. Then a faint memory stirred. She was one of the maids whom Senevier had assigned to attend her.

Yet recognising the woman did nothing to ease her fear. If anything, it made the terror spreading through her chest even worse. Her gaze darted wildly between the maid holding her down and the unfamiliar man working upon her injured leg.

No... No, I must get away!

Driven by blind panic, Talia suddenly lunged towards the maid and clawed at her face. The woman cried out and recoiled, her sun-darkened arms slipping from Talia's shoulders.

Seizing the opportunity, Talia grasped the bedsheets and desperately tried to drag herself towards the edge of the cot. But she had scarcely moved when a powerful hand seized her wrist and forced it back against the mattress. She struggled fiercely, twisting and kicking like a frightened animal caught in a trap.

"Let go of me! Get away!"

"Your Highness, please! You must remain still!" someone shouted. "We are trying to treat your injuries! If you continue moving, the wounds will tear open again!"

A man's voice thundered above her. At the same moment, the hard weight of an armoured chest pressed against her upper body, forcing her back onto the cot. Talia gasped as the pressure drove the breath from her lungs.

The restraint only made her panic worse. She twisted violently beneath him, heedless of the dreadful pain shooting through her broken leg. Every movement felt as though her bones were tearing through her flesh, yet the desperate need to escape overwhelmed all other thoughts.

"No! Do not touch me!"

"Bring the sleeping draught! Quickly!" the man shouted towards the entrance of the tent.

Talia raised her frightened eyes towards the figure looming above her. Through the haze of pain and confusion, she finally recognised his rugged features. It was her personal guard, that foolish, clumsy knight who always seemed so slow to understand anything. Yet now his expression was stern and determined, his jaw tightly clenched as he held her firmly against the bed.

A terrible feeling of helplessness swept over her. It was painfully familiar. The sensation of being trapped, of having no power over what others might choose to do to her, tightened around her chest until she could scarcely breathe.

She raised her free hand and struck wildly towards his face. "Get away from me! Let me go!"

Her voice broke into a desperate sob. The knight muttered a curse beneath his breath, but he did not loosen his hold. Talia struggled even harder, pushing against him with the heels of her hands and trying to wrench herself free.

But he quickly caught both her wrists in one powerful hand, pinning them against the mattress. With his other hand, he brought a small glass vial towards her mouth.

"Drink this, Your Highness," he urged. "When you awaken, the worst of it will be over."

Talia immediately pressed her lips together, her jaw tightening stubbornly. She would not drink it.

The knight did not withdraw the vial. Instead, he kept its rim against her tightly closed lips, his voice growing increasingly urgent.

"It is not poison! I swear it! It is only medicine to spare you the pain while they set your bones. Please, Your Highness, you must drink it!"

But Talia refused to open her mouth.

I cannot trust them. I cannot trust any of them.

Her heart pounded wildly. If she allowed herself to fall asleep, she would be utterly helpless. They could do anything they pleased while she lay unconscious, and there would be nothing she could do to stop them.

Summoning what little strength remained, Talia suddenly struck out with her elbow. The vial slipped from the knight's hand and fell away. Before he could recover it, she twisted her body upright and kicked wildly, even with her terribly injured leg, desperate to free herself from the hands restraining her.

Then the entrance of the tent stirred.

A tall, slender figure stepped quietly through the opening, and Talia's frantic movements stopped. The moment she saw his face, she froze.

Varkas.

He approached without hesitation, bringing with him the faint, cold scent of rain. Before Talia could react, his arms closed firmly around her. His hold was strong and unyielding. Drawing her back against his chest, Varkas secured her struggling upper body within the crook of one arm, leaving her scarcely any room to move.

There was no opportunity for her to pull away. Without looking down at her, he turned his head slightly towards the knight.

"Bring another draught."

The knight immediately hurried to obey, and a fresh vial was placed in Varkas's hand. He brought it to his mouth, caught the wax stopper between his teeth, and pulled it free. After spitting the stopper aside, he pressed the mouth of the bottle firmly against Talia's lips.

Talia stared at the dark liquid within the glass, her eyes widening with fear. Slowly, she raised her trembling gaze towards Varkas. His face remained unreadable. There was no softness in his expression, nor any sign that he intended to release her.

Then his voice came, low and quiet, yet carrying an authority that allowed no argument.

"Drink."
