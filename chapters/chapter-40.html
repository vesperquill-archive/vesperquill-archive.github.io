Chapter 40

Edric retrieved the cloak he had set aside earlier and gave it a thorough shake to remove the dust. He then draped it carefully over his gleaming golden orichalcum armour.

The ceremonial garment had been woven by the elves, its fine fabric decorated with ancient lettering and the embroidered emblem of a golden chalice surrounded by flames. An enchantment had been placed upon it to protect it from damage, so that even the fiercest blow could scarcely leave a mark. Unfortunately, such powerful magic seemed entirely useless against something as ordinary as wrinkles.

Edric examined the cloak with a dissatisfied frown. He carefully smoothed its creased edges, straightened the long hem, and brushed the remaining dirt from his armoured boots. Only when he was satisfied with his appearance did he rise and leave the tent.

Outside, the afternoon sky had grown dark with heavy clouds. A strong wind swept across the campsite, carrying the unmistakable scent of approaching rain. The weather had been worsening since midday, and it seemed unlikely that the skies would remain dry much longer.

Edric looked up with concern before reaching for a rain cloak. He was about to make his way towards the Second Princess's pavilion when the deep ringing of a sacred bell echoed from the temple.

The ceremony was beginning.

Edric turned sharply towards the sound. To his dismay, he saw that the Crown Prince and the First Princess had already completed their preparations. Accompanied by their attendants, they were entering the temple.

Good heavens. Has it begun already?

Without wasting another moment, Edric hurried across the campsite. He passed between the enormous stone pillars guarding the entrance and stepped beneath the temple's magnificent archway. The air inside was noticeably cooler, and a faint bluish light filled the great hall.

For a moment, he slowed his pace and searched the gathering. Servants and attendants stood quietly along the nave, while knights in ceremonial armour had taken their places beside the walls.

At the far end of the sanctuary, the Crown Prince and the First Princess stood together before the altar. The head priest had already begun reciting the prayer of consecration that would precede the sacred rites.

Edric shifted uneasily. Then he spotted Varkas standing near the side of the sanctuary and immediately hurried towards him.

"Commander," Edric whispered, stopping before him. "Her Highness the Second Princess is still preparing, but she will arrive shortly. Might we delay the ceremony for just a little while?"

Varkas turned his gaze towards him. Edric instinctively straightened.

His commander was not wearing his usual full suit of knightly armour. Instead, he was dressed in a black doublet beneath a finely crafted cuirass bearing the crest of House Sierkhan. A distinctive mantle was fastened over one shoulder, lending him an air of quiet authority.

Even Edric, who had little interest in admiring another man's appearance, could not deny that Varkas was extraordinarily handsome. For some reason, he suddenly remembered the Second Princess calling him hideous.

Well, if she has spent her life looking at a man like Lord Sierkhan, I suppose the rest of us must appear rather unfortunate by comparison.

The thought was so absurd that Edric nearly laughed. Before he could embarrass himself, however, Varkas released a quiet breath and turned towards the altar.

"Your Reverence," he said in a calm, commanding voice. "I must request that the ceremony be delayed. Her Highness the Second Princess intends to receive the consecration as well."

The priest immediately stopped his prayer. He looked towards Varkas in surprise, then glanced at Edric, who stood awkwardly behind his commander.

There was nothing unreasonable about the request. As a recognised daughter of the Emperor, Talia possessed both the right and the duty to participate in the sacred rites. Had she refused to attend, the ceremony could have proceeded without her. But since she had expressed her intention to participate, it was only proper that the others wait for her arrival.

Nevertheless, the priest hesitated. His eyes moved cautiously towards Gareth, as though seeking permission before offering an answer.

The Crown Prince's expression darkened.

"Are you seriously suggesting that I should stand here waiting for that miserable creature?" he demanded.

His voice was filled with open contempt, and he glared at Varkas as though the request were a personal insult.

"Begin the ceremony at once! I refuse to waste another moment on that girl!"

Edric felt his face stiffen. After witnessing the Crown Prince's terrible treatment of Talia the previous evening, he had not expected Gareth to agree readily. Still, he had not imagined that the prince would display such hatred before the entire assembly.

After all, the Second Princess had joined the pilgrimage by the Emperor's own command. Surely even Gareth ought to respect that.

Edric hesitated for a moment before gathering his courage.

"Forgive me, Your Highness," he began respectfully. "But Her Highness the Second Princess is a recognised member of the imperial family. By the customs of the Empire, she is equally entitled to receive the saints' blessing."

Gareth's eyes immediately turned towards him. Edric swallowed. The Crown Prince's expression twisted with anger as he strode towards the young knight.

"Have you forgotten whom you are speaking to?"

Edric lowered his head.

"Certainly not, Your Highness. I merely wished to explain—"

He never finished. Gareth suddenly seized the front of his uniform and dragged him forward with such force that the words caught in his throat.

Edric stiffened. The Crown Prince's face was only inches from his own, his dark green eyes blazing with fury. His expression was so threatening that Edric wondered whether he intended to strike him in front of everyone.

Nevertheless, he forced himself to remain still. Gareth appeared even more enraged by the knight's restraint.

"Since when does a common guardsman presume to instruct me?" he snarled. "Do you imagine yourself important enough to question my decisions?"

"Forgive me... Your Highness."

Edric struggled to speak beneath the pressure of Gareth's grip. His hands curled into fists at his sides. Every instinct urged him to seize the Crown Prince's wrist and force him to let go, but he knew perfectly well that such an action would only worsen the situation.

He was a knight of the imperial guard. And the man holding him was the heir to the throne. However unreasonable Gareth might be, Edric could not simply retaliate.

Just as the situation threatened to become unbearable, a hand settled firmly upon the Crown Prince's shoulder.

"Your Highness."

Varkas's voice was low and steady. Gareth immediately stiffened.

Edric noticed the brief change in his expression. For the smallest instant, the Crown Prince appeared startled, almost wary, before recovering his usual arrogance.

Varkas spoke again, his tone quiet but unmistakably firm.

"Many people are watching."

Gareth's face flushed. The reminder seemed to humiliate him even further, particularly because he had visibly reacted to Varkas's touch.

He released Edric's collar and turned sharply towards his commander.

"Did I not make myself perfectly clear yesterday?" Gareth hissed. "I warned you never to lay your hands upon me again. Do you imagine that my patience has no limits?"

He struck Varkas's hand away from his shoulder and stepped closer, until scarcely any distance remained between them. His breathing was harsh, and his expression suggested that he was only moments away from another violent outburst.

The knights standing nearby exchanged uneasy glances. Even Ayla began moving towards them, clearly intending to intervene before the confrontation grew worse.

But before anyone could speak, a sharp, familiar voice rang through the sanctuary.

"My, what a splendid spectacle! Had I known such entertainment awaited us, I would have made certain to attend the earlier ceremonies as well!"

Everyone turned towards the entrance. Edric looked over his shoulder. And for a moment, he forgot to breathe.

Talia was approaching the altar, accompanied by several maids.

The Second Princess, who had spent the past several days provoking one disaster after another, now moved through the sanctuary with such effortless grace that she seemed an entirely different person.

She was dressed in magnificent ceremonial attire, her appearance so carefully arranged that not a single detail seemed out of place.

A platinum-coloured bliaut flowed elegantly around her slender figure, its delicate fabric catching the pale sunlight that entered through the high windows. The light illuminated her face, revealing features that bore a striking resemblance to those of the Empress.

Edric stared in astonishment.

Good heavens...

He had known that the Second Princess was beautiful, of course. Yet seeing her dressed with such refinement made him understand why her appearance could command attention wherever she went.

She carried herself with the dignity of a woman who had been born to stand before an imperial court. And despite everything that had happened the previous evening, there was not the slightest trace of hesitation in her steps.

Edric soon realised that he was not the only one staring. Throughout the sanctuary, attendants and knights alike had turned towards the approaching princess. For several moments, the entire hall seemed to have fallen under her spell.

Curious, Edric glanced towards the Crown Prince and the First Princess. Gareth was glaring at Talia with a hatred so fierce that his expression had become almost frightening.

Ayla, meanwhile, maintained her usual composed appearance. Yet Edric noticed how tightly her fingers were clasped together. Her knuckles had turned white beneath the pressure, betraying a tension that her calm expression concealed.

The atmosphere within the sanctuary grew increasingly uncomfortable. Sensing the hostility gathering between the imperial siblings, Edric quietly moved towards Talia's side.

No one seemed particularly interested in his presence. Talia herself merely spared him a brief, dismissive glance before turning her attention towards Gareth and Varkas.

"Why have you stopped?" she asked with innocent curiosity. "I was under the impression that the two of you were about to exchange blows. Pray, don't allow my arrival to spoil the entertainment."

Gareth's jaw tightened. For several moments, he said nothing, merely grinding his teeth as he slowly stepped away from Varkas. Then his gaze travelled over Talia's elegant attire. A sneer twisted his lips.

"And whom, exactly, are you hoping to impress with such an extravagant display?" he demanded. "Do you imagine that dressing yourself in fine silk will somehow conceal the disgrace of your birth?"

Talia regarded him with a look of mild surprise.

"How very strange, Brother. Was it not you who scolded me only a few days ago for failing to dress with sufficient modesty?"

She lowered her gaze briefly towards her gown before looking back at him.

"Your criticism troubled me so greatly that I took particular care with my appearance today. I had hoped my efforts might finally earn your approval. How disappointing to discover that nothing I do can satisfy you."

Her voice was sweet and perfectly composed. But Edric had already learned that the sweeter the Second Princess sounded, the more dangerous her words were likely to become.

Talia paused, then took a small step closer to Gareth. A peculiar smile appeared upon her lips.

Edric felt a sudden chill of dread.

Surely she isn't going to provoke him again?

Only the previous evening, Gareth had nearly strangled her to death. The marks of his violence had barely begun to heal, and yet she was approaching him as though she had forgotten the danger entirely.

Edric could scarcely comprehend it.

Has she no sense of self-preservation whatsoever?

But Talia appeared utterly unconcerned. She leaned towards her brother, bringing her lips close to his ear.

Then, in a soft and almost affectionate whisper, she delivered her final remark.

"I went to such trouble to dress precisely according to your tastes, dear Brother..."
