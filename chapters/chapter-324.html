Chapter 324

She let out a heavy sigh and pulled her hood down over her forehead. Just then, the horse suddenly changed direction.

Talia looked at him in confusion. Lucas had left the main road leading to the castle gates and was riding along the edge of the forest.

“……Where are we going?”

When she glanced back with an anxious expression, Lucas's face, with one hand tightly gripping the reins, was staring silently straight ahead.

Talia caught sight of his face. As he guided the horse along a narrow path beside the castle walls, Lucas answered in a curt tone.

“There is no reason to cause an unnecessary commotion, is there? There is a small gate behind the castle walls. If we use that entrance, we should be able to enter the castle without attracting anyone's attention.”

Talia's eyes widened at his unexpected words.

She had assumed that he intended to drag her through the castle gates and humiliate her before everyone.

Why was he trying to protect her?

For a moment, she regarded him with suspicion. Then, remembering that Lucas's fiancée had recently visited the castle, she furrowed her brow.

With guests from outside currently staying at the castle, news that the Grand Duchess had left without permission would undoubtedly disgrace the Sierkhan family.

It was clear that Lucas intended to quietly conceal her attempted escape to prevent such an unfortunate incident.

Just as Talia had expected, no unnecessary commotion arose as they entered the castle.

“I have not informed the servants that you left the castle, Sister-in-law. I also told the maids inside that I had asked you to accompany me on an outing, so there was no need for them to worry. You should not encounter any trouble over this incident.”

Lucas spoke as he brought his horse to a halt behind the castle.

Talia looked up at him, utterly bewildered.

She could understand why he would want to preserve the family's reputation in front of their guests. But why would he go so far as to protect her reputation as well?

Whether he noticed the suspicion in her eyes or not, Lucas turned his head and issued a stern warning to the knights following behind them.

“You are all to remain absolutely silent about this incident as well. Remember, if even the slightest rumor begins to spread, I will hold every single one of you responsible.”

With that, Lucas swiftly dismounted and extended a hand toward her.

“Take my hand. I'll help you down from the horse.”

Talia frowned.

She had not the slightest desire to accept his assistance, but with her injured leg, it was clear that jumping down from the horse would only leave her sprawled awkwardly on the ground.

After hesitating for a moment, Talia finally took his hand.

Lucas pulled her toward him, prompting her to grasp his shoulders. He then wrapped both arms around her waist, lifted her effortlessly, and set her down on the ground.

Talia immediately stepped away from him.

The feeling of having his body pressed so closely against hers had made her uncomfortable.

Lucas's attitude, as he extended his hand toward her, made her inexplicably uncomfortable.

“I can manage on my own now. Go and attend to your own affairs.”

Talia replied curtly and turned away. However, before she could take even a single step, Lucas caught her arm once more.

“I hope nothing like this ever happens again.”

Lucas tilted his head toward her and continued in a low voice.

“The suspicion that you have been communicating with heretics has not yet been completely cleared, Sister-in-law. You know very well what people will say if word gets out that the Grand Duchess secretly left the castle again under such circumstances, don't you?”

Talia lowered her eyes, her expression unreadable.

The Sierkhan family would undoubtedly continue to suspect her of conspiring with heretics. If that was the case, what reason did she have to conduct herself cautiously simply to avoid their scrutiny?

Despite these thoughts, she had no desire to engage in a pointless argument, so she obediently nodded.

“I was careless. I'll make sure nothing like this happens again.”

Lucas looked slightly surprised by her subdued response. After a moment, he spoke in a noticeably gentler tone.

“If you wish to take Khan outside, please let me know. Unless I have something particularly important scheduled, I'll accompany you myself.”

Talia's eyes widened at his unexpected offer.

Lucas was the brother in law who had always treated her with disdain.

Why was he suddenly making such an offer?

What could he possibly be thinking?

When she openly looked up at him with suspicion in her eyes, Lucas added, almost as though he were making an excuse.

“Since you know the terrain around here about as well as you did today, it would be better if I accompanied you from the beginning.”

“There's no need for that. From now on……”

Talia, who had been about to say that she intended to refrain from going outside, abruptly closed her mouth.

The thought of keeping Khan confined within the castle walls forever was unbearable. After what had happened today, surely she understood better than anyone just how suffocating life inside the castle must be for him.

Biting her lower lip, she turned to look at Khan.

Ordinarily, the wolf would have growled and revealed his hostility the moment Lucas approached her. Yet, for some reason, he was now sitting quietly a short distance away, merely watching them.

An inexplicable sense of unease rose within her at his unusually calm behavior.

It was almost as though the wolf were about to leave her at any moment.

Talia instinctively held out a hand.

“Khan, come here.”

At her call, the wolf, who had been sitting perfectly still as though lost in thought, slowly rose and approached her.

Only then did Talia let out a small sigh of relief. Turning back toward Lucas, she nodded.

“……All right. The next time I need to go outside, I'll let you know.”

Perhaps pleased with her answer, a gentle light softened the man's face. It was a warmth she had never once seen in his expression before.

Talia lowered her eyes, feeling bewildered. An unfamiliar emotion stirred within her, leaving her strangely uneasy.

Suppressing the feelings that had unsettled her, Talia pressed her lips together and led Khan toward the rear entrance of the castle.

She could feel Lucas's gaze following her from behind. Nevertheless, Talia never once turned around.

He gazed down at her face with eyes filled with helplessness.

The pale moonlight streaming through the window dimly illuminated her face, which was buried in a nightmare.

As he listened helplessly to her faint sobs and the words of apology slipping between her parted lips, he soon lowered his head and carefully licked her cheeks, damp with sweat and tears.

Talia buried her face in the fur around his neck like a frightened young animal seeking refuge in its mother's embrace.

Her slender fingers weakly clutched at his thick fur.

Feeling her sorrowful breaths gently dampen his skin, he stared into the pitch-black darkness stretching beyond the window.

Every night, he desperately wished for the hours to pass even a little faster.

During the day, she struggled to maintain her composure...

Whenever Talia, who struggled to maintain her composure during the day, fell asleep, she would inevitably be tormented by nightmares. The knowledge that there was absolutely nothing he could do for her as she suffered plunged him into an unbearable sense of helplessness.

He could not protect her from the misfortunes that threatened her. No, worse still, he felt as though every desperate attempt he made to protect her only brought her closer to the terrible fate that awaited her.

He no longer knew what he ought to do.

Would it not be better to simply leave her side as things were?

He closed his eyes tightly.

The thought that it might be better for him to disappear, so that he would never again find himself attacking Lucas, refused to leave his mind.

But if he abandoned her here alone, and something like what had happened in the past were to occur again… who would be there to save her then?

He trembled faintly and buried his face in the fur at the nape of her neck. As he recalled the sight of Talia lying lifeless upon the bed, an agonizing pain swept through him, as though his spine were being torn apart.

Even now, she was a woman who occasionally repeated dangerous and reckless actions with an innocent expression. If he were to leave her side, she might fall apart completely.

If Khan had not been there to protect her, I would surely have committed another foolish mistake.
