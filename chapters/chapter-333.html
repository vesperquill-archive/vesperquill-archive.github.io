Chapter 333

Before long, he came across a narrow stream flowing down a gentle slope.

He stopped at the water’s edge and looked carefully at the terrain on the other side. Several rocks, much taller than a grown man, stood evenly spaced apart. He recognized this as one of the places he had taken note of while searching for a route to escape the castle with Talia.

Only then did he have a rough idea of where he was. If he continued east a little farther, he would reach a hill overlooking the northwestern walls of Raedgo Castle.

He pushed off with his hind legs and leaped across the stream in a single bound. To his own surprise, he soared high into the air before landing lightly on the ground in a graceful arc. As he picked up speed, the scenery on either side rushed past him.

What surprised him was that despite running so vigorously, his breathing remained perfectly steady. Considering that he had suffered nearly fatal injuries and spent a long time in a barely conscious state, it was an unbelievable change.

After running nonstop, he finally came to a halt near the edge of the forest and looked down at his thin front legs. Perhaps because he hadn't eaten properly for some time, he had lost so much weight that his bones were visibly protruding. Yet, for some inexplicable reason, rather than feeling weak, he was filled with a vitality unlike anything he had ever experienced before.

Suddenly, the events that had taken place just before he lost consciousness came back to him. The moment he saw the man’s sword coming toward him, an unknown power lying dormant within him had erupted violently.

As he recalled what had happened, he decided to experiment by focusing his attention on the heat circulating throughout his body. The heat concentrated around his heart gradually began to expand, as though responding to his will. He then tried releasing it outside his body.

At that moment, thousands of invisible, delicate threads seemed to spread outward from him. At the same time, the range of his perception expanded rapidly, and unfamiliar sensations flooded his senses like a rising tide.

Feeling his mind grow hazy, he squeezed his eyes shut. It was as though the nerves throughout his body were extending beyond its physical boundaries, stretching endlessly in every direction.

Overwhelmed by the dizzying sensations, he briefly curled up. However, he soon realized that he could control how much of the outside stimuli he received and focused his mind once more. As he drew back some of the energy that had spread in every direction, the overwhelming sensations clouding his mind gradually subsided.

Once the splitting headache eased, he tried sensing the presence of the living creatures scattered around him. Not far away, he could sense several fairly large beasts roaming the forest in search of food. He extended a thin thread toward the nearest creature and wrapped it around it. The beast immediately stopped moving.

As an experiment, he tried calling the creature to his side. Before long, a direboar emerged from behind the bushes.

He approached the creature and carefully examined its dark red eyes. The direboar’s eyes were strangely calm.

He quietly observed the beast, which obediently waited for his instructions as though it were facing a superior member of its own species. He then gestured with his eyes toward a nearby tree. In response, the direboar slowly walked toward it.

This time, he mentally instructed it to move in the opposite direction. The creature immediately turned around and approached him again.

After spending some time making the direboar move back and forth, he ordered the creature to lie down at his feet before tearing into the back of its neck with a single bite. Even as it drew its final breath, the creature offered little resistance.

Without hesitation, he tore off chunks of the direboar’s flesh and greedily devoured them. Despite being brimming with energy, his body, which had gone without food for so long, still needed nourishment.

Having devoured nearly half of the enormous wild boar in no time, he licked the blood from around his mouth and continued on his way.

It didn’t matter why he had suddenly gained such a power. After all, everything that had happened to him so far had been a series of inexplicable events.

Questioning it wouldn’t necessarily give him any clear answers. It was better to focus on what he could accomplish with this power instead.

Setting aside the countless questions running through his mind, he raced effortlessly through the densely packed birch trees.

Bathed in the winter light, Raedgo Castle looked as sturdy and imposing as ever. He climbed to the top of the hill and carefully observed the activity along the castle walls and watchtowers. At first glance, everything seemed perfectly peaceful. However, there was no telling what was happening inside.

He cautiously moved to a spot where he had the clearest view of the main castle. If possible, he wanted to get closer to the castle walls and find out what was happening inside. However, approaching in broad daylight would only draw the attention of the guards.

He paced restlessly among the bushes, anxiously scratching at the ground.

How was she doing now? Was she safe?

He stared intently at the castle walls until his eyes became bloodshot, struggling to swallow as his throat grew increasingly dry.

The Zram people had invaded Kalmor around the time late spring gave way to early summer.

Although he had no idea exactly how long he had been unconscious, if the events of his previous life were repeating themselves, she would have been driven out of the main castle by now and would be staying in the annex.

As he recalled what she had been like back then, a pain as sharp as having his flesh carved away swept over him. Hers was a life she had barely managed to cling to amid countless betrayals and painful losses. He could not even bear to imagine how much she must be suffering as she struggled through each day, having lost all hope in life.

After anxiously pawing at the snow for a while, he finally stepped onto the overgrown grass. He simply could not bring himself to wait until sunset.

Keeping his body as low as possible, he moved along the slope, staying out of sight of the watchtower. However, beyond the gentle hillside lay a vast, open field with almost nowhere to hide.

Eventually, he stopped at the foot of the slope and tried releasing the energy within his body toward the castle walls, just as he had done in the forest earlier.

The invisible threads swiftly stretched across the open field and reached beyond the stone walls.

Before long, he began to sense the presence of countless living beings. He could faintly make out the movements of soldiers patrolling the castle walls, knights gathered at the training grounds, and servants bustling back and forth between them.

However, his senses could not reach the inner castle. The farther his energy extended, the weaker the sensations became, until he could barely detect the presence of living beings at all.

After thinking for a moment, he extended a thin thread of energy toward one of the soldiers standing on the castle walls. He wondered if he could influence human behavior in the same way he had controlled magical beasts.

However, no matter how hard he tried, the thread repeatedly bounced off the soldier’s body, as though blocked by a hard shell.

After several attempts, he soon realized that he could not control humans. He changed direction and began searching for other living creatures wandering near the castle walls.

Fortunately, he managed to take control of a small bird circling above the castle walls.

He sent the small bird flying toward the annex behind the inner castle.

At that moment, an unfamiliar scene appeared before his eyes, overlapping with his own surroundings. Overcome by a sudden wave of dizziness, he staggered. Constantly moving images became mixed with the motionless objects around him, throwing his senses into confusion. Instinctively, he braced himself with his front paws and squeezed his eyes shut. As he did, one of the two overlapping scenes disappeared.

To his astonishment, what remained in the darkness was a panoramic view of Raedgo Castle from high above. The castle walls lined with soldiers quickly receded from view, and the snow-covered rooftops of the buildings appeared one after another.

He soon realized that he was sharing the bird’s vision and quickly focused on controlling its movements. With nothing more than a mental command indicating which direction to go, the bird skillfully changed course and descended in a wide, sweeping arc toward the courtyard behind the inner castle.

Before long, the annex came into view. As he slowly circled the building, he spotted Edric Lubon chopping firewood in the backyard. He immediately descended and perched on a nearby tree branch.

With his sleeves rolled up, the man skillfully swung his axe. It seemed he had simply found something to keep himself occupied while passing the time.

After observing the seemingly relaxed man for a moment, he moved toward a window that offered a clear view of the bedroom.

Edric Lubon's presence here meant that Varkas Raedgo Sierkhan had already left Kalmor.
