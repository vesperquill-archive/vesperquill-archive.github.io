Chapter 43

Talia stared in horror at the enormous black silhouette rising against the fading light of the setting sun.

The creature crouched low upon the ground, its massive body covered in dark, gleaming scales. Then, with a force that seemed capable of shaking the earth itself, it sprang upwards. At the height of its leap, its enormous reptilian head turned towards the people below, and a pair of crimson eyes glowed with a terrifying light.

Talia's knees weakened beneath her, and she collapsed onto the ground. The beast released a scorching breath before spreading its immense wings, their black membranes stretching across the sky like a curtain of darkness. For one dreadful moment, it seemed as though the entire world had been swallowed by night.

Varkas immediately drew his sword, his commanding voice cutting through the growing panic.

"Prepare the suppression equipment! At once!"

The order had scarcely left his lips when several knights rushed forward through the terrified crowd. While servants and attendants fled in every direction, the soldiers quickly surrounded the enormous beast and drove heavy iron stakes into the ground, securing thick chains between them.

Moments later, dozens of steel harpoons were launched towards the creature's massive body.

Talia clapped her hands over her ears as a deafening roar erupted from its throat. The beast threw back its enormous head and struggled violently against the chains, its movements sending tremors through the earth.

The iron stakes groaned beneath the strain. Several were torn free from the ground and hurled into the air as the monster twisted its immense body in fury.

"Fall back! Everyone, retreat!"

The warning rang across the camp, but it came almost too late.

With a violent movement, the creature broke free from its remaining restraints and swept one of its enormous wings across the campsite.

Talia screamed.

Two tents were destroyed in an instant, their wooden supports splintering beneath the force of the blow. Several servants were thrown through the air as though they weighed no more than scraps of paper.

One of them landed near Talia, the body rolling across the dirt before coming to a dreadful stop. Its limbs were twisted at unnatural angles, and the sight made her stomach turn.

For a moment, she could scarcely believe what she was witnessing. It was as though she had suddenly awakened inside a nightmare.

"Bring more suppression equipment!"

Varkas's fierce command cut through the chaos and brought Talia back to her senses. She struggled to rise, forcing strength into her trembling legs. But the moment she looked towards him, the blood drained from her face.

Varkas was charging directly towards the beast.

His movements were so swift that he appeared almost like a flash of lightning crossing the battlefield. With his sword drawn, he raced towards the creature's open jaws, seemingly indifferent to the fact that its enormous body stretched more than thirty kvet in length.

Talia's heart nearly stopped.

"Varkas!"

Her desperate cry had scarcely escaped her lips when the monster lashed out with one of its wings.

Talia stood frozen, her eyes widening in terror.

The enormous black wing swept across the sky, blotting out the last traces of sunlight. In the next instant, it descended upon Varkas, completely concealing him from her sight.

For one dreadful heartbeat, Talia could see nothing but darkness.

Then a pale flash of steel tore through the black membrane.

Varkas's sword sliced through the leathery wing as easily as a blade passing through parchment. With another powerful stroke, he severed the thick muscles connecting it to the creature's body.

The monster released an agonised roar. Deprived of one of its wings, it staggered and began to collapse towards the side.

Varkas did not waste the opportunity.

With practised speed, he drew a chain sickle from his belt and hurled its curved blade towards the creature's open mouth. The hook caught deep within its jaws, lodging among the rows of sharp, needle-like teeth.

Dropping into a low slide, Varkas caught the chain against one of the iron stakes driven into the ground. Then, gripping it firmly, he pulled with astonishing strength.

The hook pierced through the creature's lower jaw. The enormous beast was dragged violently downwards, crashing onto the earth like a great fish caught upon a fisherman's line.

Talia could only stare in disbelief.

Varkas possessed the slender, graceful build of a dancer, yet the strength he had just displayed seemed almost beyond human understanding. He had brought down a monster of such tremendous size through sheer skill and overwhelming force.

Holding the chain taut with both hands, he turned towards the surrounding knights and shouted.

"Now! Finish it!"

The soldiers immediately obeyed.

A relentless shower of steel harpoons descended upon the fallen creature, piercing its thick scales and burying themselves deep within its flesh.

The beast thrashed desperately, its enormous body shaking the ground as it struggled against the attack. Yet with its wing severed and its jaws restrained, it could no longer defend itself.

More harpoons struck. Soon, the creature's black body bristled with steel, resembling some enormous, monstrous porcupine.

At last, its movements weakened. Its long tongue slipped from between its jaws, and its great body collapsed motionless upon the bloodstained earth.

Only then did Talia realise that she had been holding her breath. She inhaled sharply, her chest aching as air finally returned to her lungs.

"Your Highness! Are you injured?"

A knight of the Imperial Guard hurried towards her, his sword still drawn. His face was filled with concern, and judging by his hurried appearance, he seemed to have crossed the battlefield simply to find her.

Upon reaching her, he sheathed his weapon and extended a hand to help her rise.

But Talia barely acknowledged him. She brushed past his offered hand and hurried towards Varkas.

He was already gathering the equipment used in the battle, calmly winding the loosened chain around his arm as though he had done nothing more remarkable than complete an ordinary task.

When he noticed her approaching, he raised his head. His cold gaze travelled over her from head to toe, examining her for injuries with the detached attention of a man fulfilling an assigned duty.

Ordinarily, such indifference would have wounded her pride. But at that moment, Talia could think of nothing else but whether he had been hurt.

"Are... are you all right?" she asked breathlessly. "Have you been injured?"

Her eyes moved anxiously over his face and shoulders before stopping at a dark crimson stain upon his neck.

Talia's heart tightened.

Without thinking, she reached towards him and brushed her fingers against the bloodstained skin.

For a dreadful instant, she feared that the creature had wounded him. But the blood did not belong to Varkas.

Relief swept through her so suddenly that she almost felt faint.

Then she became aware of the strange silence between them. Talia slowly lifted her eyes.

Varkas was staring at her. His eyes were unusually wide, and for once, his expression seemed to have lost its customary composure.

Talia immediately withdrew her hand. A cold sweat broke across her skin.

What have I done?

She had spent years striking him, insulting him, and hurling whatever objects happened to be within reach whenever her temper overcame her. Now, without warning, she had approached him like some anxious lover, worrying over the possibility that he might have been hurt.

He must think me utterly ridiculous.

Flustered, she searched desperately for an excuse.

"I... I merely thought that if someone I have known for ten years were to perish before my very eyes, it would be rather unpleasant," she stammered. "Such a sight would undoubtedly disturb my sleep. I have no desire to be troubled by your ghost in my nightmares."

Varkas regarded her in silence for a moment. Then he replied in his usual dry, cutting manner.

"...How fortunate, then. I have no intention of troubling Your Highness's dreams."

Strangely enough, the familiar sharpness of his reply brought Talia an unexpected sense of relief. She allowed her aching shoulders to relax.

At least he was speaking to her as he always did. At least he was alive.

Varkas appeared about to say something further when another terrified scream rang out from the far side of the camp.

Talia turned towards the sound. Her breath caught in her throat.

The sky was filled with black wings.

Several enormous shapes were descending through the evening clouds, their dark silhouettes spreading across the heavens like a gathering storm.

The knight standing nearby stared upwards, his voice trembling with disbelief.

"Why... why would a flock of wyverns appear at this time of year?"

Varkas immediately regained his commanding composure.

"Save your questions for later!" he ordered sharply. "Bring out every piece of suppression equipment we have! Your Highness, come with me."

Before Talia could protest, he seized her wrist and began pulling her across the campsite.

She hurried after him, struggling to match his long strides. Her legs moved so quickly that her feet began to ache, but she dared not slow down.

Around them, the camp had descended into complete chaos.

The air was thick with smoke, heat, and the metallic smell of blood. Flames rose from shattered tents, while servants and soldiers fled from the enormous creatures descending upon them.

Carriages were overturned and crushed beneath the monsters' weight. Wooden supports snapped like dry twigs, and the screams of the injured mingled with the deafening roars of the wyverns.

Talia stared at the devastation with growing horror.

Destruction...

It was the very catastrophe she had wished for.

For so long, she had prayed that some terrible misfortune might befall the imperial procession. She had imagined disaster sweeping through the camp, destroying the peace and happiness of those she despised.

And now that disaster had finally arrived.

Yet there was no satisfaction in witnessing it. No pleasure. Only a cold, overwhelming terror that seemed to seep into her very bones.

"Lord Sierkhan! The barricade is ready!"

A knight's voice rang through the smoke.

Varkas immediately changed direction, leading Talia towards the centre of the camp.

There, dozens of heavy supply wagons had been arranged in a great defensive formation. Their reinforced frames were linked together, creating a makeshift fortress behind which servants, attendants, and other frightened members of the procession had gathered.

Varkas guided Talia into the protected area and released her wrist.

"Remain here," he instructed firmly. "Whatever happens, you are not to leave this place."

Talia looked around in confusion. The frightened crowd pressed together behind the barricade, while beyond the wagons, the sounds of battle continued without pause.

Varkas gestured towards the defensive formation.

"These wagons have been fitted with protective enchantments. You will be safe behind them."

Talia turned towards him.

"And what of you?"

But Varkas offered no answer. He had already begun turning away.

A sudden panic seized her. Talia caught hold of his arm before he could leave.

"Have you taken leave of your senses?" she demanded. "Where do you think you are going?!"

Varkas glanced down at the hand gripping his sleeve.

"The protective wards will not endure indefinitely," he replied. "I must reduce their numbers before the barrier gives way."

He carefully but firmly removed her fingers from his arm. Then, without another word, he turned and ran back towards the battlefield.

Talia stood motionless. For several moments, she could only stare at his retreating figure, watching as he disappeared towards the smoke, the flames, and the monstrous shapes descending from the sky.

Then her heart gave a violent throb.

No...

She could not bear the thought of him returning to that dreadful place. Not when she had only just seen how close he had come to being killed. Not when another flock of monsters was waiting beyond the barricade.

Before she could stop herself, Talia rushed after him.

"No! Don't go!"

Her voice broke as she called out desperately.

"Varkas! Varkas!"
