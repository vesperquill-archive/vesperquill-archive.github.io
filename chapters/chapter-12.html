Chapter 12

With the first light of morning, Talia rose from her bed and approached the looking glass. For a considerable while, she stood before it in silence, studying the pitiful figure reflected upon its polished surface. Her cheeks had grown hollow, her complexion was unnaturally pale, and the fine bones of her face appeared far too prominent beneath her skin. The little princess who had once taken such pride in her beauty now scarcely resembled the lively child she remembered.

Good heavens. I look no better than that wretched sewer rat they served in my stew.

The thought was so bitterly absurd that a dry laugh escaped her lips. There was little amusement in it, however, for she knew perfectly well what had brought her to such a miserable condition. Yet as she continued to regard her reflection, something unfamiliar began to settle within her expression. The frightened little girl who had spent so many nights weeping beneath her coverlet was no longer willing to remain entirely helpless.

At that moment, the door opened, and the maid ordinarily entrusted with her morning toilette entered the chamber. The woman carried herself with the same careless confidence she had displayed throughout the preceding months, evidently expecting her young mistress to submit without complaint.

“You have risen rather early this morning, Your Highness. Come, then. I shall see that you are properly washed and dressed.”

“I have no need of your services.”

The reply was delivered so coldly that the maid paused in surprise. Her sharp eyes narrowed with displeasure, and she regarded Talia with the superior expression of one who had grown accustomed to exercising authority over a child.

“Your Highness would do well to remember that an imperial princess must maintain a faultless appearance at all times. Such matters are governed by the customs of the court, and these childish displays of obstinacy are most unbecoming—”

“Nanny!”

The sudden cry silenced her at once. Without troubling herself to hear another word, Talia brushed past the astonished maid and seized the small bell beside her bed. She rang it so vigorously that its shrill sound echoed throughout the chamber.

“Nanny! Attend me this instant!”

A considerable commotion arose in the adjoining room, and presently the nanny appeared in the doorway, looking thoroughly disordered. She had evidently been sleeping far beyond the proper hour, for her eyes were swollen with drowsiness and her manner betrayed the confusion of someone summoned from a particularly comfortable slumber.

Talia pointed towards her with an air of imperious certainty.

“From this morning onward, my nanny shall have sole charge of my washing and dressing. Her Majesty the Empress has granted permission for this arrangement. You may therefore consider your services unnecessary.”

The maid hesitated, clearly uncertain whether such an order ought to be obeyed.

“But, Your Highness, I was instructed—”

“Do you presume to question a command issued with Her Majesty's authority?”

Talia's voice rang sharply through the chamber. The maid stared at her for a moment, evidently surprised by the sudden alteration in her manner. Yet the prospect of disputing an imperial command, particularly when the young princess appeared determined to defend it, was not an agreeable one. With a stiff bow and an expression of ill-concealed resentment, she withdrew.

Talia watched until the door had closed behind her. Only then did she turn towards the nanny, who remained standing near the entrance, rubbing her sleepy eyes as though she could scarcely comprehend what had occurred.

“You heard my instructions, I trust. From this day forward, my toilette shall be entirely your responsibility. I expect you to attend to your duties with greater care than you have shown thus far.”

“Very well, Miss…”

The reply was accompanied by an enormous yawn.

Before the woman could finish, Talia raised her hand and struck her sharply across the cheek. The sound of the slap broke the stillness of the room, and the nanny staggered slightly, staring at her young mistress with an expression of utter astonishment.

Talia did not lower her gaze. Instead, she drew herself up and assumed the cold, commanding expression she had so often observed upon Senevia's face whenever the Empress was displeased.

“How many times must I remind you of the proper manner in which you are to address me? Have you grown so careless that you have forgotten my station altogether?”

The nanny's brown eyes widened, and her mouth opened without producing a sound. Talia looked directly into her startled face, pronouncing each word with deliberate severity.

“From this moment onward, you shall address me as Your Highness. Should you forget yourself and call me Miss again, you may expect another correction of precisely the same nature. Have I made myself sufficiently clear?”

The woman nodded hurriedly, one hand pressed against her stinging cheek.

“Y-Yes, Your Highness.”

“Then cease standing there in astonishment and attend to me. I have already wasted quite enough of the morning.”

The nanny obeyed, although her movements were so slow and uncertain that the business of washing, arranging Talia's hair, and dressing her in suitable clothing consumed several hours. By the time the last fastening had been secured, Talia felt so faint that she feared her legs might fail beneath her. Weeks of insufficient nourishment had left her terribly weak, and even the effort of remaining upright demanded considerable determination.

Nevertheless, she refused to betray her exhaustion. Drawing her shoulders back, she lifted her chin and walked towards the door with all the dignity she could summon.

Upon entering the corridor, she discovered a stranger standing beside her chamber.

He was a tall man dressed entirely in black, with a gloomy countenance and an air of such profound stillness that he might almost have been mistaken for a shadow. His expression was unreadable, and there was something unsettling about the quiet manner in which he regarded her.

For an instant, Talia stopped in surprise. Then she remembered her mother's promise to provide her with a capable guard, a man whose obedience had supposedly been secured through years of severe discipline.

So this must be the useful fellow Mother spoke of.

She examined him briefly before giving a small, imperious movement of her chin, indicating that he was to accompany her. The man offered no objection and followed at a measured distance as she made her way towards the dining hall.

Several servants were waiting when she arrived, their expressions suggesting that they had grown impatient with her unusually late appearance. Some exchanged displeased glances, while others made little effort to conceal their annoyance. Talia noticed every look, but she gave no indication that their behaviour concerned her.

Instead, she walked to the end of the long dining table and seated herself with deliberate composure. Resting her hands upon the polished surface, she regarded the attendants as though their presence existed solely for her convenience.

“You may serve my breakfast now.”

The command was delivered with quiet authority. Nevertheless, the servants lingered for several moments, their faces betraying the indifference with which they had long treated her wishes. Only after an uncomfortable delay did they begin carrying the dishes into the room.

A maid of approximately thirty-five years supervised the preparations. She directed the other attendants with practised efficiency, ensuring that the silver plates were arranged in their proper order. At length, a bowl of thick soup was placed before the young princess.

Talia lowered her eyes towards it.

The pale broth contained beans and pieces of meat, while a layer of white cream floated upon its surface. To an unsuspecting observer, it might have appeared an entirely ordinary breakfast. Yet Talia had learned through bitter experience that the most innocent-looking dishes could conceal the vilest surprises.

She took up her spoon and began stirring the soup. Her movements were slow and deliberate as she pushed aside the larger ingredients and scraped along the bottom of the bowl. Presently, the spoon encountered something heavier than the surrounding food.

Talia slipped it beneath the object and raised it towards the surface.

A dead sparrow emerged from the thick liquid, its slender neck twisted at an unnatural angle. The little creature had evidently been dead for some time before being thrown into the soup, for its body had already begun to decay. Worse still, several thin, pale maggots writhed within its ruined eye sockets.

A violent sickness rose in Talia's throat. For one terrible instant, she feared that she would vomit upon the table, just as she had done on that dreadful morning several weeks earlier.

But she would not permit it.

She pressed her lips together, forced herself to remain still, and waited until the first wave of nausea had passed. Then she raised her head and fixed her gaze upon the maid who had supervised the serving of the meal.

“You there. Come forward and take the seat beside me.”

The woman looked startled by the unexpected command. Her eyes moved briefly towards the bowl before returning to Talia's face, and a faint uneasiness disturbed her carefully composed expression.

After a moment's hesitation, she inclined her head.

“I beg Your Highness's pardon, but my duties require my attention elsewhere. There is still a considerable amount of work to be attended to.”

Having offered this excuse, she turned as though to leave the dining hall.

Talia watched her with narrowing eyes. She recognised the woman perfectly well. On previous occasions, the same maid had remained nearby after the food was served, observing her young mistress with an expression of quiet satisfaction whenever some dreadful object was discovered upon the plate.

Yet today she appeared strangely anxious to depart.

So she knows what is in the bowl.

The conclusion came to Talia with absolute certainty.

Before the woman had taken more than a few steps, Talia rose abruptly from her chair and seized the heavy brass pitcher standing upon the table. Gathering what little strength remained in her weakened body, she swung it towards the maid who had dared to turn her back upon an imperial princess without permission.

The metal struck the woman's head with a dreadful sound.

A sharp scream escaped her lips, and she collapsed upon the carpet. Though the blow had been delivered by a child whose strength had been diminished by hunger, the weight of the pitcher was sufficient to inflict a serious injury.

The dining hall fell into immediate silence.

Nearly a dozen servants stood motionless, staring at the fallen woman in disbelief. Several maids raised their hands to their mouths, suppressing cries of horror, while others appeared incapable of understanding what they had just witnessed.

Talia alone remained composed.

She set the pitcher aside and turned towards the black-clad man who had followed her into the room. He stood near the wall, observing her with the same gloomy expression he had worn outside her chamber.

“You. Bring that woman here and seat her beside me. At once.”

For a moment, the guard merely looked at her. Then he stepped forward without a word, bent down, and seized the injured maid beneath her arms.

The woman had begun to recover her senses, and she struggled weakly as he lifted her from the floor. Her resistance, however, proved utterly useless against the strength of a trained man. Despite her frightened protests, he dragged her towards the table and forced her into the chair beside Talia.

The young princess resumed her own seat and examined the woman with an expression of cold indifference.

The spout of the brass pitcher had torn the skin upon the maid's scalp, and dark blood was trickling down her temple. Two crimson trails marked her pale cheek, while her eyes moved about the room in a confusion of pain and terror.

Talia appeared entirely unmoved by the sight.

With deliberate care, she pushed the bowl of soup towards the trembling woman. The dead sparrow remained visible amongst the thick broth, its ruined body resting upon the surface.

The maid's gaze fell upon it, and the little colour remaining in her face disappeared.

Talia placed a spoon into her hand, closing the woman's damp fingers around its handle.

“In consideration of the admirable diligence with which you have attended to my meals, I have resolved to extend to you a small token of my gratitude. You shall have the honour of sharing my breakfast this morning.”

She spoke with such perfect composure that the words might almost have been mistaken for a gracious invitation.

“I trust you will not offend me by refusing so sincere a gesture. You are to finish the entire bowl, without leaving so much as a single spoonful.”

“Y-Your Highness, I… I cannot…”

“Eat.”

The command was delivered with unmistakable severity. Talia caught the woman's trembling hand and guided it towards the bowl.

“You considered this dish suitable for my consumption, did you not? Surely you cannot object to partaking of something which you were so willing to place before your princess.”

“I… Your Highness, I merely…”

The maid's lips trembled violently. Her eyes darted towards the other servants, silently appealing for assistance, but no one moved. The attendants who had so often regarded Talia with contempt now stood frozen in bewilderment, uncertain how to respond to the extraordinary change in their young mistress.

Talia's expression hardened.

“I commanded you to eat. Must I repeat myself?”

The woman recoiled and attempted to pull her hand away. In her desperation, she pushed against the table and struggled to rise, but the black-clad guard immediately restrained her, holding her firmly against the chair.

Unable to escape, the maid looked from the man's gloomy countenance to Talia's pale face. Her earlier arrogance had vanished entirely, replaced by a terror so profound that tears began gathering in her eyes.

“Y-Your Highness… I beg your forgiveness! I have behaved most wickedly, and I know that I deserve your displeasure. But I swear upon my life that such a thing shall never happen again. Please, I implore you, show me mercy just this once!”

Talia regarded her without the slightest change in expression.

“If you fail to empty this bowl, you shall not leave this dining hall upon your own two feet.”

The maid went deathly pale.

Her eyes dropped towards the guard's waist, where a sword hung within its scabbard. She had seen the weapon when he entered, but only now did its presence acquire a dreadful significance.

Her breathing grew ragged, and she began shaking uncontrollably.

“Please… Your Highness, I beg you! Have mercy upon me!”

“I am showing you mercy,” Talia replied with chilling calm.

She leaned back slightly in her chair, studying the terrified woman as though the matter were perfectly simple.

“Were I so inclined, I might order your death this very instant. Yet instead, I have chosen to grant you an opportunity to preserve your life. Surely you ought to be grateful for such generosity.”

The maid could offer no answer. Her entire body trembled, and the spoon rattled faintly against the edge of the bowl as she struggled to keep her hand steady.

Talia pushed the soup closer until it stood directly before her.

The decaying sparrow floated amongst the pale broth, its dreadful appearance leaving no doubt as to what the woman was being commanded to swallow.

“Now, if you have properly understood the kindness I have extended to you…”

Talia's voice remained soft, but there was nothing gentle in her expression.

“Then put every last morsel into your mouth.”
