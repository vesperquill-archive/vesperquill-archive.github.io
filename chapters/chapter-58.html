Chapter 58

Talia's back straightened as though a bolt of lightning had struck her. For several moments, she could only stare at the woman standing beside her bed, scarcely believing what she had just heard.

The Head Maid turned gracefully towards the attendants and continued, "Her Majesty the Empress has expressly commanded that Your Highness be dressed and presented in the finest possible manner, so that you may appear before the Supreme Sovereign of the Empire with the dignity befitting your station. I must therefore ask that you cooperate with us."

At a slight gesture from her hand, the four maids standing nearest the bed stepped forward, each carrying an assortment of carefully arranged garments. Talia immediately drew back, her expression hardening with resentment.

"All of you, get out. I have not the slightest intention of meeting a man like him."

"Your Highness would do well to refrain from such insolence."

Although the woman's voice remained soft, its former gentleness had vanished. Her words were as sharp and cold as a blade covered in winter frost.

"Imperial Princess though you may be, I shall not tolerate such reckless remarks concerning His Imperial Majesty. I will overlook your discourtesy on this occasion, but I advise you to guard your tongue henceforth."

Talia flinched at the reprimand, but her indignation quickly overcame her surprise. Narrowing her eyes, she glared at the woman.

"And if you choose not to overlook it, what then? Will you run straight to your precious Emperor and report me?"

"I most certainly could," the Head Maid replied without the slightest warmth.

A dry, humourless laugh escaped Talia's cracked lips. "Indeed? Then by all means, go to the Main Palace this very instant and tell him! Tell him that his ill-mannered Second Princess is shouting at the top of her lungs that she has no desire whatsoever to behold his precious face!"

A sharp crack suddenly interrupted her.

Pain flashed across the back of her hand, and Talia froze in astonishment. For a moment, she could scarcely comprehend what had happened. Then she slowly raised her eyes towards the woman standing before her.

The Head Maid was lowering a slender switch, her steel-coloured gaze utterly devoid of sympathy.

"The dignity and privilege Your Highness enjoys as an imperial princess are bestowed solely by His Majesty the Emperor," she declared coldly. "I shall not permit you to speak of him in such a disgraceful manner."

"How dare you..." Talia whispered, her voice trembling with disbelief. "How dare you raise your hand against me...!"

She could say no more. Her entire body shook with such violent rage that the words caught in her throat. Yet the Head Maid merely regarded her with detached indifference before turning towards the door.

"Her Majesty the Empress has given explicit instructions that Your Highness is to be brought before His Imperial Majesty, regardless of the means required. Do you truly wish to compel us to use force?"

With those words, she threw the door wide open.

Talia's breath caught. Beyond the threshold, a line of armed soldiers stood waiting in the corridor, their uniforms bearing the insignia of the Empress Palace.

For several moments, she could only stare at them. The Head Maid watched her reaction with quiet satisfaction, seemingly taking pleasure in the colour draining from the princess's face.

"There is no need to make this affair any more unpleasant than necessary," she continued smoothly. "If Your Highness cooperates, everything shall be concluded before long."

The meaning of her words was unmistakable. If Talia refused, they would drag her from the room by force.

She clenched her fists so tightly that her knuckles turned white, while hot tears of humiliation gathered in her eyes. If Senevier had been standing before her at that very moment, Talia felt she might have seized a dagger and driven it straight into the woman's heart.

How dare she... Was it not enough that she had already taken everything from me? Must she now strip away the last miserable remnant of my pride as well?

Her jaw tightened as hatred burned fiercely within her chest.

I shall never forgive you. Never, for as long as I live.

"Hurry along," the Head Maid instructed, turning towards the attendants. "See that Her Highness is properly washed."

At her command, two maids approached the bed. They had scarcely reached towards Talia when she struck their hands away.

"Do not touch me!"

Her scream rang through the chamber, causing the maids to recoil as though they had narrowly escaped a shower of sparks. Fear was plainly written upon their faces. Whatever authority the Head Maid possessed, they were evidently still terrified that the princess might remember their actions and seek revenge upon them later.

Noticing their hesitation, Talia immediately seized upon it.

"My nanny shall bathe me. The rest of you will leave my room at once."

The Head Maid studied her for several moments before releasing a weary sigh. "Very well. Your nanny may attend to your bath. However, I shall personally oversee your dressing. On that matter, Your Highness, I will not yield."

With those words, she gestured towards the door and ushered the other maids from the chamber.

A short while later, Nanny came hurrying inside. The moment Talia saw that familiar face, an unexpected rush of relief swept over her.

This was the very woman whose thoughtless remarks had wounded her so deeply, who had spoken of her ruined appearance with such careless cruelty. Yet now, surrounded by strangers who treated her with cold authority, Talia felt almost overcome with gratitude merely to see her.

When Nanny approached to help her rise, Talia instinctively buried her face against the woman's shoulder. She remained there for several seconds, struggling to contain the anguish swelling within her chest.

Nanny, however, appeared entirely unaware of her distress. Her face was bright with excitement.

"Your Highness, did you hear what the Head Maid said? His Imperial Majesty has summoned you! Why, he must be terribly worried about you after learning of your injuries!"

Talia said nothing, allowing the woman's cheerful chatter to pass over her without listening. All her attention was devoted to the painful effort of standing.

The moment she placed weight upon her injured leg, a deep ache shot through the bone. She gritted her teeth as anger flared within her.

Walking without difficulty, indeed! What nonsense had those healers been speaking?

Her ruined leg could scarcely support the weight of her own body. Every attempt to move sent another unpleasant throb through the damaged joints. Nevertheless, she forced herself upright and began dragging her aching limb towards the folding screen.

Nanny supported her as she made her way to the bathing area, where Talia carefully lowered herself into the marble bathtub. Warm water was poured over her shoulders, its gentle heat gradually seeping into her stiff muscles and offering a little relief from the constant discomfort.

For a brief moment, she allowed herself to relax. Then her gaze fell upon her leg.

Beneath the clear water, the thick scar stood out in an ugly shade of reddish purple. Its swollen, irregular shape reminded her of some dreadful, bloated worm clinging to her flesh.

Talia stared at it with growing disgust. Almost without thinking, she reached down and began picking at the raised skin with her fingertips.

A sharp slap landed against her back.

"Stop that!" Nanny scolded.

Talia immediately withdrew her hand. A wave of sorrow rose within her, but she said nothing. She was afraid that if she protested, Nanny might lose her temper and leave her alone with the strangers waiting outside.

And so she lowered her head and remained silent.

"Now then, lean your head backwards," Nanny instructed. "I must rinse your hair."

Talia obeyed, resting her head against the edge of the tub while Nanny lifted a large pitcher and poured warm water over her hair. The water streamed down her scalp, carrying away the remaining traces of soap.

Once the washing was finished, Nanny helped her from the bath. Talia rose unsteadily, her weakened legs trembling beneath her. A large towel was wrapped around her body, and Nanny carefully dried her before dressing her in fresh undergarments and a clean petticoat.

"There we are," the woman said with satisfaction. "The Head Maid may attend to everything else."

Having completed her task, Nanny gathered her things and hurried from the room with surprisingly light steps. Talia watched her go, feeling a strange emptiness settle within her chest.

A moment later, the Head Maid returned, accompanied by her attendants.

Talia stood quietly before them as Trania Meldren examined her from head to foot with a cool, appraising gaze. The woman walked slowly around the princess, studying her appearance as though inspecting an article that had been presented for approval.

Talia endured the scrutiny without speaking.

After circling her several times, Trania clapped her hands. "Arrange Her Highness's hair in an elegant braided style and dress her in the gown we have brought. I shall select the jewels myself."

At once, the maids sprang into action.

It seemed as though dozens of hands descended upon Talia simultaneously. She stood motionless while they handled her like a lifeless doll, every touch feeling like another small torment.

Three or four maids attended to her clothing, fastening one garment only to remove it again when the arrangement failed to satisfy the Head Maid. Meanwhile, the others began working upon her hair.

Her long locks, which reached almost to her waist, were divided into numerous sections, carefully braided, and arranged into an elaborate style. Pins were inserted one after another to hold the intricate plaits in place.

Talia scarcely moved. She had neither the strength nor the desire to resist. She merely allowed them to turn her this way and that, raising her arms when instructed and standing still while they adjusted the fabric around her.

At last, after examining the finished arrangement with a critical eye, Trania gave a small nod.

"This will suffice."

The maids who had been smoothing the folds of Talia's skirt immediately stepped away. Talia looked towards the Head Maid with weary eyes as Trania approached carrying a cape of pearl-coloured silk.

The woman draped it carefully over the princess's shoulders, adjusted the fastening, and stepped back to inspect her work one final time.

Then she turned towards the door.

"It is time."

Talia hesitated, remaining where she stood for several moments before reluctantly beginning to walk.

She tried desperately to move with her former grace, but her scarred, half-numb leg would not obey her as it once had. Each step felt awkward and unnatural, and she could neither bend her knee properly nor place her weight upon the limb without discomfort.

As she entered the corridor, she became painfully aware of the soldiers standing along the walls. Their eyes seemed to follow her every movement, and her face burned beneath their scrutiny.

Stop looking at me.

She wanted to turn upon them and demand that they avert their gaze. Indeed, in that moment, she felt an almost overwhelming desire to tear out every pair of eyes that dared witness her humiliation.

But she could do nothing.

She merely clenched her teeth and continued walking until, at last, she emerged from the Separate Palace.

A carriage stood waiting near the garden. Beside it was an unfamiliar knight clad in armour.

Talia glanced at him, a faint frown forming upon her brow.

What became of the previous one?

For a moment, the face of the knight who had irritated her throughout their journey came to mind. Had he resigned from his position, or had he simply been assigned elsewhere?

She considered the matter briefly before dismissing it.

What does it matter? He was no different from any other guard.

Such men came and went, replaced as readily as servants with the changing seasons. Perhaps it was simply time for another to take his place.

With an aloof expression, Talia approached the carriage and climbed inside. The effort sent another sharp ache through her leg, but she refused to show it.

Trania entered after her and took the seat opposite. For several moments, the Head Maid remained silent, her lips pressed into a thin, disapproving line.

Then she spoke.

"Your Highness, His Imperial Majesty intends to discuss a matter of considerable importance with you today. It is therefore essential that you conduct yourself with proper dignity. You must take care with your words and observe the decorum expected of an imperial princess."

Talia looked at her without enthusiasm.

"And what, precisely, is this matter of such great importance?"

"I have not been informed of its particulars."

Talia raised an eyebrow.

"Then how can you possibly know whether it is important at all? For all you know, it may be nothing more than some tiresome nonsense."

Trania's lips tightened. For a moment, she appeared on the verge of replying, but then thought better of it and closed her mouth.

Her patience was plainly wearing thin.

Talia turned away just as the woman raised a hand to press her aching temples. A faint sense of satisfaction stirred within her as she directed her gaze towards the carriage window.

Before long, the vehicle began to move.

The gardens of the Separate Palace gradually disappeared behind them as the carriage travelled across the vast grounds of the Imperial Palace.

Talia watched the familiar scenery pass with growing unease. Her hand drifted towards her injured knee, which had already begun to throb.

Even with the thick cushions beneath her, the constant jolting of the carriage sent painful vibrations through the damaged joints of her leg. Each uneven movement seemed to strike deep into the bone.

Talia pressed her lips together as cold perspiration gathered upon her forehead. She discreetly wiped it away and swallowed the pain without complaint.

She would not give the Head Maid the satisfaction of seeing her suffer.

After some time, the carriage began to slow, finally coming to a halt before the Main Palace.

Talia descended with considerable difficulty. The moment her feet touched the ground, bright sunlight poured over her, forcing her to narrow her eyes.

She stood upon the broad plaza before the Main Palace, where an unusually large crowd had gathered.

Talia looked out across the assembly with hollow eyes.

Why are there so many people?

Before she could give the matter further thought, Trania spoke from beside her.

"His Imperial Majesty is waiting, Your Highness. Come."

Talia bit her lower lip and began walking.

Almost immediately, she became aware of the people turning to look at her. Their hushed voices drifted through the air, and whispers followed her as she crossed the plaza.

She tried to ignore them.

Keeping her chin raised, she made her way towards the main entrance with as much dignity as she could muster. But before she could pass through the doors, a guard hurried forward and stepped into her path.

"You cannot enter through here at present," he said apologetically. "I must ask that you use the side entrance instead—"

His words abruptly faltered.

He had finally looked properly at her face.

Recognition dawned, and the guard drew a sharp breath.

Talia fixed him with a gaze as cold as winter.

"Do you know whom you are attempting to bar from entering?"

The man's face drained of colour.

"F-Forgive me, Your Highness!"

He immediately stepped aside, bowing so hastily that he nearly stumbled.

Talia swept past him without another word. Several onlookers hurriedly bowed as she passed, their movements awkward and belated, but she paid them no attention.

Crossing the threshold, she entered the grand hall.

By then, her entire body was damp with perspiration. The effort of concealing her limp had exhausted her far more than she cared to admit.

And despite everything, she knew she had failed.

No matter how carefully she moved, her injured leg continued to betray her. She could feel the unevenness of her steps, and surely everyone else could see it as well.

Talia glanced warily towards the people gathered around her, watching them from the corner of her eye.

Then something unusual caught her attention.

Standing upon one side of the hall was an enormous birdcage, so large and imposing that she wondered how she had failed to notice it upon entering.

Her gaze moved towards its interior.

And suddenly, her eyes widened.

"...What is that?"
